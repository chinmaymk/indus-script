No. 13 (BM. 122841, diam. #f in,, ht. #in.), Found at
Ur in the season 1929-0, without context. Materially the
seal is of the usual type. The face is quartered by four
forked branches springing from the angles of a small square,
with some internal decoration, in the middle. In each of the
four spaces thus formed is an uncertain decoration, which
might be an animal head, except for the circle with centre-
spot which can be seen in them. The device seems purely
decorative.

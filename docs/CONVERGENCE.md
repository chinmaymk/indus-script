# Convergence: strongest surviving interpretation

28 September 2026. **No demonstrated decipherment.** The most defensible working interpretation is a structured system of short formulaic inscriptions, with some stroke groups plausibly representing quantities. That interpretation does not identify the language, establish that every text is an account, or supply words and sounds for all 417 signs.

## Priority verdict

| Theory | Evidence and present verdict |
|---|---|
| Repeated strokes contribute quantities; neighboring forms distinguish contexts or units | Best partial reading candidate: repeated counts and substitution patterns provide testable constraints. Photographs distinguish long and stacked arrangements. Quantity equality, arithmetic operations, marker meanings and actual units remain unverified. |
| Recurrent beginning/ending signs organize formulaic labels | Strong structural support from position and neighbor distributions, including within-site contrasts. “Title,” “institution,” “owner” and grammatical suffix are competing interpretations, not translations. |
| Fish-family signs identify a commodity or production domain | Weaker semantic lead. Selected archaeological associations survive only with substantial context uncertainty, and other signs give comparable local contrasts. Fish = gemstone and a particular pronunciation are not established. |
| A specific phonetic/language key accounts for every sign | No verified key in this investigation. Structural compatibility does not choose among languages or label meanings. All 417 semantic and phonetic entries remain unassigned. |

The ranking prioritizes testability and evidential support, not numerical probabilities. It synthesizes the [main findings](REPORT.md), [inventory review](ALL_INVENTORY_NEXT_TEST_REVIEW.md) and [local association controls](CHANHU_LOCAL_GRAPHICS_AND_SPECIFICITY.md).

## What the last numerical test actually established

[H-326 and H-924 photographs](BALANCED_TABLET_PRIMARY_REVIEW.md) corroborate a three-long versus three-stacked contrast. Let a be a long stroke's effective contribution beside W700, b a long stroke beside W840, and c a stacked stroke beside W840. **If the paired faces denote equal quantities**, H-306 gives 3a=2b and the balanced examples give 3a=3c. A positive solution is a=c=1, b=3/2. Thus c/b=2/3 is a conditional consequence, not a deciphered conversion.

The still-secondary H-1923 comparison adds 2a=2b and contradicts that system. Removing it is a sensitivity test, not evidence that its transcription is wrong. Artificially merging long and stacked classes also creates a contradiction. H-925 was not independently photographed in this phase; the three balanced rows repeat one numerical relation. W740 remains an unexplained residual sign, so the equations do not explain even all signs on these tablets.

The [exact graph result](../results/numeral_class_constraints.json) was independently checked by rational Gaussian elimination. Its scope is five equations selected by an earlier frozen screen, not all inscriptions or all 417 symbols. H-924 also has an identity correction: the secondary excavation number3286 belongs to the next primary catalogue row, H-925(1); the primary H-924 number is unknown.

Earlier candidate numerical keys (L,S,v)=(10,8,6) and (36,20,4) are conditional fits. Relaxing their shared-value assumption permits [infinitely many solutions](NUMERAL_IDENTITY_SENSITIVITY.md). Neither key should be presented as the reading.

## Decision under the remaining usage constraint

Stop broad searches, repeated internal fits and speculative all-sign assignments. Preserve the compact working hypothesis above. A newly authenticated, commensurate quantity pair would distinguish numerical keys; the existing [calibration design](NUMERAL_CALIBRATION_DESIGN.md) specifies the test. No such pair is currently established. For the weaker production-domain hypothesis, the concrete acquisition target is Penn collection1064 field cards and registers, as specified in the [route audit](RESEARCH_ROUTE_AUDIT.md).

Four final H-1923 searches found a bibliographic lead to a combined1994/1995 report volume, but no target photograph or authenticated figure join; see the [search record](H1923_REPORT_SEARCH.md). That search is parked. No outreach was sent.

The complete decipherment goal remains unresolved. Mathematical consistency proves only what follows from explicit assumptions; it cannot supply the missing ancient meanings.

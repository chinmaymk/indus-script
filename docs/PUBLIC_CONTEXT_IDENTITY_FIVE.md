# Five-record catalogue identity check

11 October 2026. **Five specific CISI field associations are documented; three
also match individually inspected Mackay seal-table rows.** No general suffix
parser, semantic anchor or replicated context contrast has been established.

The first five Mohenjo-daro Lipi rows with nonmissing CISI and excavation
identifiers were fixed in ACTIVE_STATE before inspecting the primary tables.
No inscription outcomes were used for selection or linkage. Original Lipi
identifiers remain unchanged, and the earlier zero-overlap strict profile remains
valid under its original normalization rule.

CISI I printed365–366 and II printed434 explicitly define column1 as CISI,
column2 as Finnish Concordance and column3 as excavation number. The FC column
is not automatically Mahadevan's catalogue. M-584 and M-592 have separate FC
values0779/0749; M-1140 has a distinct FEM plate reference188. These observations
explain why the composite Lipi strings cannot be normalized by guessing a common
suffix. They establish individual catalogue associations, not the exporter’s
general serialization or newly inspected object markings. CISI itself warns
that some excavation identifiers may be wrong.

The original Mackay table pixels agree with the public CSV on the three selected
rows. No188 records block13, houseIII, room17, level−8.0ft. No209 records block7,
houseIII, room39, level−8.2ft. No3 is a surface find with blank locus. Thus these
two numeric context records are at different loci and supply no matched deposit
comparison. Printed369 also explicitly describes SD3225 in a separate narrative:
SD block6, room13, level−0.8ft, a tablet appearing bronze. This is not a fourth
seal-table row; its area and depth convention remain separate. DK4807's primary
context was not authenticated in pixels in this review and remains unresolved.

Two primary pages were retrieved and registered with bytes, SHA256 and provenance;
the third was already retained. Sandbox DNS failures received no body and are
preserved in the fetch records. An initial page-offset lead was corrected after
viewing retained printed363: chapter PDF45 refers to printed369, not363. The
uncorrected offset was never used as evidence for a context value.

Independent read-only criticism confirmed the five targets and the column/suffix
limitation. The [machine-readable review](../results/public_context_identity_five.json)
pins every source and render, retains both unmatched targets and reproduces only
the three manually authorized field substitutions as a separate metadata join.
No source corpus is amended. The public CSV remains unauthenticated elsewhere;
depth and room metadata alone do not establish original use, contemporaneity,
independent deposits or meanings.

# Primary-image review of selected India-volume ending examples

The retained CISI photographs support the modified-fish presence and ordered
comb–crossed-sign–fish grouping in M-81 and L-89. M-133 provides a photographed
control with a different second sign and both a modified fish and a plain fish.
L-9, added as a proposed W526 alternate-ending comparison, is visibly broken at
the comb end. It cannot independently establish intentional W526/W527 variation.
This is source-exposed visual review, not a blind transcription or physical autopsy.

## What is actually supported

- **M-81:** Six encoded sign units are compatible with the six positions visible
  in the impression. Its left-to-right image order is W527, W555, W240, W002,
  W617, W070. W617 comprises the visually doubled gridded form; two visible
  grids must not be counted as two independent corpus tokens. The third sign is
  a modified fish, consistent with the export's W240. The first comb has its teeth
  to the left in the impression, to the right in the original. The crossed second
  sign is distinct from the adjacent fish. The face has surface wear and rounded
  edge irregularities, but no gross missing corner that requires an extra lost sign
  is apparent. This supports keeping the positive fish status. It does not prove
  pristine preservation or semantic completeness.
- **L-89:** The original and impression show a compact rectangular text-only
  face with five units, compatible with W527 W555 W235 W002 W861 in the
  impression's left-to-right image order. The comb and crossed second sign
  precede a modified fish, then the short strokes and the diamond with interior
  mark. The outline is rounded/worn, without an obvious substantial missing
  text-bearing corner. A fish-positive classification is supported. No text–image
  commodity relationship is available because there is no separate animal motif.
- **M-133:** The impression's first comb, second diamond/crossed sign and two
  successive fish forms agree with W527 W550 W240 W220. The second sign
  differs visibly from the W555 form in M-81/L-89; the modified fish and plain
  fish follow it. The longer remainder is compatible with the export sequence,
  but this targeted review does not independently certify every fine allographic
  distinction. The upper edge and first comb are worn and contrast is poor in the
  impression; the original helps. No grossly missing text-bearing corner is
  apparent. The object is a useful positive control for fish outside the exact
  W527 W555 ending, not a semantic counterexample by itself.
- **L-9:** Its original upper-right corner and impression upper-left corner are
  missing. The loss meets the terminal comb. The crossed sign and modified fish
  remain recognizable, consistent with the latter two units of W526 W555 W235.
  The photographs do not securely determine whether the surviving comb was
  intentionally W526 rather than damaged W527, nor rule out extra lost text.
  The raw complete=Y flag must not establish a complete three-sign inscription.
  A fish-positive observation survives this uncertainty; exact-ending and
  full-sequence comparisons need a damaged-edge sensitivity or exclusion.

Image order here is a spatial description, not a newly proven reading direction.
Under the export's reported right-to-left direction, the comb pair is an ending.
Numeric W labels come from the existing transcription system; this review checks
its selected shape classes and order against photographs, rather than deriving an
independent sign inventory. No semantic or phonetic values are assigned.

## Reproduction and navigation log

All sources were already retained; no downloads were needed. Full-page renders,
OCR navigation aids and tight crops are in `results/primary_pages/`. Render
parameters are in `results/ending-india*_extraction.json`; the crop script
[`render_ending_india_crops.py`](../scripts/render_ending_india_crops.py) records
fractional source coordinates and preserves the images' orientation without
retouching. Evidence hashes are in
[`ending_india_review.json`](../data/processed/ending_india_review.json).

attempts. Correct pages are70,79,295,276. The first M-81 crop missed its original

to the Pakistan-volume review and is not represented as inspected here. L-51's
previous primary review is reused without another purported independent review.

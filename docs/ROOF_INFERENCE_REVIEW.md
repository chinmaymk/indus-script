# Review of the roof inference

**None of the reviewed evidence refutes a common semantic contribution of the roof. Neither does it establish one.** The variants limit exact graphical invariance; the corpus screen establishes absence of a specified contextual witness pattern in its selected data; the K-29 photographs authenticate one physical roofed-stroke occurrence. These are different findings and must remain separate.

This review reads the frozen results and source-review records, without rerunning or altering their selections, counts or transcriptions. It adds no meaning, sound or new catalogue equivalence. The non-fish variant drawings were inspected directly in the preceding assigned review; other physical observations below are attributed to the named primary-source reviews, not presented as a second independent image audit.

## Which claims can be rejected

The literal claim that each selected base code always denotes one exact geometric outline is contradicted by the documented variants. In particular, M162’s upper branching and prong counts change, while M137 can have additional short arm marks. A code-wide operation defined as adding a roof to an otherwise fixed three-prong U drawing is therefore too strong. The broader possibility of adding a comparable roof to variable realizations of a base remains open. This distinction does not depend on whether the editorial variants were semantically equivalent in antiquity.

The finite claim that this frozen selected export supplies the preregistered opposite-state rectangle fails: it contains zero qualifying candidates. That does not reject a productive modifier in general. It rejects neither a common semantic value nor even a context-sensitive shared function, because those hypotheses do not entail that this small, selected export must contain these exact complete-frame configurations. The M092 branch has no eligible complete roofed field; it supplies no opportunity for that branch’s contrast.

A claim that graphic similarity or substitution alone identifies meaning is logically unsupported. Distinct signs can share a syntactic slot, and synonymous, homophonous, orthographic, numerical or nonlinguistic alternatives can produce similar observations. None of those specific explanations has been selected by the current evidence.

## Inferential traps and limits found

1. **The rectangle endpoint is deliberately narrow.** It requires exclusive plain/plain and exclusive roof/roof states in different frames. A shared operation could occur in mixed frames, depend on context, or leave no exact-frame overlap in this sample. Failure of this endpoint is not a necessary-condition failure for common semantics. Conversely, even a positive rectangle could reflect independently contrasting choices in a common formula.
2. **“Only” is observed exclusivity.** Plain-only and roof-only describe this export under its exclusions, not ancient prohibitions. No linguistic minimal pair or obligatory modifier rule follows from these labels.
3. **Code state is not an image-level feature annotation for every occurrence.** Variant rows support the selected coarse distinction where inspected, but do not independently authenticate every roof/plain label in the context screen. Exact shape changes and loss of separation can be hidden by normalization. These new variant observations are not a retrospective recoding of the frozen screen.
4. **Uninformative nulls remain uninformative.** The reported invariant scores in three shuffle settings and the small attainable set in the fourth do not measure evidence for or against a semantic modifier. More shuffles cannot repair missing contrasts or establish historical exchangeability.
5. **Repeated references are not automatically independent objects.** The frozen report explicitly preserves this caveat for4407/5444. The four-corner distinct-reference rule is a screening condition; original-object and die checks are still needed. Likewise, unknown secondary site labels are not matching authenticated provenances.
6. **Complete first fields do not make complete objects equivalent.** The reported `_ M176` witnesses have differing other sides. A same-frame comparison is limited to that field. Neither concatenating those sides nor discarding their differences would create a semantic control.
7. **Variant classes cannot prove allography.** The Appendix documents Mahadevan’s groupings. It does not establish same sound/meaning among their members, and still less between separately numbered base and roofed signs. The evidence also does not prove that allography is impossible.
8. **Graphic stroke counts are not numeral values.** Three visible long strokes under a roof and two nearby short strokes can be described without assigning3,2,5, a multiplier, a place value or an arithmetic operator. No independent quantity anchor is supplied here.

These are limits on extrapolation, not detected contradictions in the frozen report’s stated conclusion. Its central wording—insufficient qualifying contextual evidence, common function unresolved—is appropriately calibrated. The later artifact and variant work strengthens source authentication and bounds the graphic model; it does not change the frozen numerical endpoint.

## The next test actually enabled

**Timing update:** The concurrently completed [roof-X identity review](ROOF_X_IDENTITY_REVIEW.md) has now inspected the original EH407 photograph and authenticated 4407 as excavation1722. Its roof-state prediction therefore cannot now be preregistered or presented as a fresh future replication. The proposed checks below retain their original design scope, but the original-image follow-ups now concern4493,5477 and the exact5444 photograph. The5444/3027(2) photograph remains unresolved; unsuffixed3027/EH558 is not an authenticated substitute. This note relies on that concurrent review and does not claim a second image inspection here.

The immediate test is **source-level replication of the selected `_ M176` witness labels and object independence**, not another significance calculation. Its finite references are already known:4407 and5444 are reported as M138 M176,4493 as M066 M176, and5477 as M060 M176. The source review supplies old-publication/photograph locators and records the additional sides. That makes a bounded, falsifiable check possible without inventing new sign values.

Before examining the original images, freeze three predictions for these four references:

- The predicted target group has the recorded roof state—roofed for4407/5444/4493 and unroofed for5477—with sufficient preservation to classify it. A clear contradictory shape fails that local prediction; an obscured group is unassessable rather than confirmation.
- The adjacent M176-shaped group and the complete first-field extent are independently supported. A hidden additional sign, lost region or wrongly identified field invalidates that reference’s use as a complete exact-frame witness.
- Documentary identifiers distinguish or connect the physical objects and dies. If4407 and5444 resolve to the same object or die, they form one dependent evidence unit; separate catalogue numbers cannot rescue independence.

Preserve image-level roof shape, spacing/attachment and all other sides as observations separate from token labels. Report all four outcomes, including failures and unassessable photographs. This test could falsify a particular local catalogue-to-image interpretation or its independence premise. Passing would strengthen the mixed-frame evidence; it would still leave the plain-X corner absent and would not establish the frozen rectangle or a shared semantic effect. Since the targets are known, call it source replication, not a blind discovery.

A genuine next **distributional prediction** is conditional on additional authenticated support. The [design review](MODIFIER_TRANSFER_DESIGN_REVIEW.md) proposes holding out a graphic root, controlling object/die and collapsed-formula leakage, and predicting roof/plain state from context using a fixed baseline and feature set. It requires at least three eligible roots with both states and multiple independent groups per state. The present evidence does not establish those gates; the exact rectangle screen’s sparse support is not itself a formal power assessment for every alternative model. Check the gates before fitting. If they fail, record “not estimable.” If they pass, failure rejects that specified contextual prediction model, while success supports transferable contextual information—not an identified sound, quantity or meaning.

A semantic hypothesis would need an additional independent anchor and a prediction that distinguishes its proposed value from rival explanations. The current reviews supply no such anchor, so no semantic test with a defined numerical or linguistic outcome is yet enabled.

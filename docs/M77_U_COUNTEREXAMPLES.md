# Plain U (M328): primary counterexamples and matched-text comparisons

**M328 occurs outside a universal stroke-group-plus-U pattern.** Complete lines put it initially, medially, finally after a complex nonstroke sign, and alone on a separately numbered side. These facts reject that strict universal positional claim. They do not reject every possible quantity or measure interpretation of U.

A requested pair, **4373 / 4702**, has identical main text and identical three-stroke-plus-U side-lines. A further comparison, **4548 / 4509 / 4508**, does have matching main text with two, three and four long strokes before U. The positive and negative comparisons are kept separate.

The M328 concordance begins in the lower half of PDF575 / printed564. That page supplies the initial-U and damaged-edge leads. PDF592 / printed581 includes many stroke+U endings, but also the visibly longer side-lines of 4448, 5460 and 5461. The latter's original Texts entry was checked explicitly above. A page containing mostly final-U examples is not evidence that every listed U is final.

**The solus example needs a precise limit.** Text 4401 side 2 has metadata `20003`, confirming a one-sign line. Its side 1 has `310003` and M089, a group of three long strokes. Thus it defeats a mandatory preceding stroke group *within U's line*, but remains compatible with a relation between signs on different sides. Such a relation must be specified and tested; the source does not license simply concatenating sides.

**Damaged examples cannot supply negative or positive counts.** In 4639 and 5456 the lost span could contain strokes, other signs, or several signs. Neither is a complete solus-U line, and neither can be restored to a specific stroke+U expression. The scan of 4639's original Texts is PDF135; OCR elsewhere misleadingly recognized a different reference as 4639.

For 4373 / 4702, concordance PDF626 / printed615 and original Texts PDF121 / printed110 and PDF138 / printed127 agree. Both originals print `210001` for side 1 and `20001` for side 2: type 2, side numbers 1 and 2, field 00, line 0, original direction 1 on each side. The canonical records reproduce the main side and omit the U side-line. This pair repeats the same pattern; it cannot demonstrate a variable quantity.

For the three-state comparison, original Texts PDF130 / printed119 (4548) and PDF128 / printed117 (4509 and 4508) print `310001` and `20001` throughout: type **3, miniature tablet**, sides 1 and 2, field 00, line 0, original direction 1 on both sides. The main glyphs were compared with supplied M048, M342 and M176. The side-lines visibly contain two, three and four full-height strokes and plain U. This is a controlled structural contrast compatible with a variable quantity associated with U. It does not identify U's meaning, a unit, counted commodity, spoken language or actual numerical values.

## Identity, evidence and limits

Plain M328 has no internal stroke or terminal decoration. Explicitly labelled Sign List PDF45 / printed34 distinguishes it from M329, which has short internal top strokes. Sign List PDF46 / printed35 distinguishes decorated M342. These distinctions are visible within 1206 and 8202 themselves. The stroke groups M087, M089 and M095 have two, three and four long strokes respectively; “two/three/four” here describes morphology, not an independently deciphered numerical value.

The evidence ledger is `data/processed/m77_u_counterexamples.json`. It includes primary metadata, canonical excerpts, sign-image hashes, primary page hashes and reproduction commands. Durable source renders use `results/primary_pages/m77-u-counterexample-*.png`; the paired detail images are `m77-u-counterexample-pair-detail.png` and `m77-u-counterexample-4508-4509-detail.png`. The original source remains `data/raw/mahadevan_1977_text.pdf`, SHA-256 `6dcdd074a0174e5fab149590fbbf82342711d345764e9763f8e5e92c81cf7f99`.

The main audit comprises 10 selected references, 13 reviewed lines, 33 identified positions and two unknown spans. The separate three-state comparison adds three references and six complete lines. These are purposeful witnesses, not a random sample or a population frequency estimate. Checks concern normalized published transcription rather than artifact photographs; casting relationships and physical-object independence were not established. No phonetic values were assigned. No canonical data, strict dataset or shared analysis was changed, and no source was downloaded.

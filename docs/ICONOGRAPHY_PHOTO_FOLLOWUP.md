# H-598: a photographed transfer target

**The H-598 text and animal pairing is now photographically supported.**
[Parpola (2009), figure 5a, printed p.49/PDF13](https://tuhat.helsinki.fi/ws/portalfiles/portal/127256488/Parpola_A_2009._Hind_leg_fish_in_the_Indus_script._Scripta1_37_76.pdf)
reproduces a modern impression explicitly identified as H-598. I downloaded the
author's public university copy and inspected the rendered page. The image places
the seven-group inscription above a four-legged, long single-horn animal and a
stand, on **the same impression face**. The [render](../results/primary_pages/iconography-photo-parpola-13.png)
and [provenance ledger](../data/processed/iconography_photo_followup.json) preserve
the check.

The rightmost compound has U-shaped stems with leaf-like tips and an internal
pincer-like figure, consistent with the previously located Mahadevan5073 drawing.
This supports the target side of the [transfer audit](ICONOGRAPHY_THEORY.md).
It does not establish a precise Wells-code variant: the local feature catalogue's
P118 row groups W777, W778 and W782. Nor has the W777 reading on copper donor M-604
been independently authenticated. Thus the complete copper-to-seal equivalence
remains partly dependent on the literal export. The image also does not establish
the meaning of the export's `Bull1:W` suffix or an ancient name for the animal.

## Checking a published warning about H-598

[Daggumati and Revesz (2021), p.5](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41599-021-00713-0.pdf)
report an ICIT orientation error involving H-598 and H-1924. Their subsection's
pictured glyph is a bent/Z-like sign with opposed end strokes, **not the compound
at the right of H-598**. This distinction was verified in the
[rendered publisher PDF](../results/primary_pages/iconography-photo-allographs-5.png).

The local catalogue describes that shape in P188, whose Wells codes are W435/W436.
In this pinned export, H-598 and H-1924 both use W435; other objects named in the
same discussion use W436. This identification is an inference from shape and
contexts, not a numbered Wells citation in that paragraph. The export may already
reflect the correction. No source transcription was changed. In particular,
the warning does not supply grounds to remove or replace W777.

## Scope and access

This bounded follow-up located H-598; M-1803 remains photographically unverified.
The new image checks an existing association rather than introducing a reading.
It also supersedes the earlier statement that no H-598 photograph had been
inspected; the earlier limitations for M-1803 and the copper donor still apply.

The Harappa-hosted PDF download returned 403, so the public university copy was
used. The Nebraska repository download also returned 403; the author's public
publication list provided the working publisher PDF link. Neither denial was
bypassed. Three downloaded files—the two papers and the author's publication
index—are in `data/raw/`. URLs, byte counts and SHA256 hashes are in the ledger for
registration. Rendering used the existing PDF inspection script; no new
statistical test or claim of decipherment is made.

# Fish-like signs and bead production: what the deposit records permit

28 September 2026. **A small, conditional area comparison favors fish-sign
presence in the reported production complex, but the records still do not
support a matched deposit test or a gemstone reading.** This phase also located
the expedition's catalogued field-record archive, a concrete source for resolving
the remaining archaeological joins. No sign meaning or phonetic value is assigned.

The [protocol](CHANHU_DEPOSIT_PROTOCOL.md) was saved before new grouped counts.
Three independent reviews covered archaeology, the complete illustrated-seal
frame and inference design. Previously published site-wide counts were already
known, so this is source-exposed development work throughout.

## A finite artifact frame, including unresolved objects

The original catalogue's Plates LI–LII contain **65 entries**. All have an
excavation number, locus and signed elevation; 60 have a square and five instead
have a trench. This is the complete declared illustrated section, not all seals
ever excavated. Broken or unreadable objects are retained. [Register review](CHANHU_DEPOSIT_REGISTER_REVIEW.md),
[machine-readable register](../data/processed/chanhu_deposit_seal_register.json).

The metadata-only join to the secondary corpus yields 55 exact number/suffix,
plate/item and compatible-class matches; seven have no candidate, two have
different excavation numbers at the same plate reference, and one is classified
as MISC in the secondary source. Five accepted matches have seven disagreements
in square, locus or elevation. In particular, original LI26 is **3043**, whereas
secondary C62 repeats **3817**, the number of original LI25; original LII15 is
**2531**, versus secondary C64's **2431**. Two further signed-level corrections
are LI12 **+7.0** rather than +7.2 and LI31 **+14.9** rather than +14.1.
Original images were checked independently. No conflicting record was silently
merged or deleted. [Join output](../results/chanhu_deposit_metadata_joins.json),
[independent verification](CHANHU_DEPOSIT_JOIN_INDEPENDENT.md).

All 74 secondary site rows have phase recorded as `-`. Exact metadata matches
do not independently authenticate their inscription transcription, and a lack
of detected disagreement does not validate untested fields.

## Why the strict deposit comparison is not yet available

The excavation's [recording-method description](CHANHU_RECORDING_METHOD_REVIEW.md)
explicitly carries numbered positions down through successive occupations.
Thus equal locus numbers do not establish equal deposits or dates. The new
[archaeological screen](CHANHU_DEPOSIT_EXPOSURE_REVIEW.md) records 21 overlapping
contexts, including production evidence outside 9/D, alternative crafts and
qualified comparison candidates. They are not 21 independent deposits.

Court190 supplies an additional explicit unfinished-bead floor at +10.4 ft,
but the declared seal frame contains no entry at locus190. The best lower-evidence
comparison is bath442: floor/drain +6.7 ft, with seals described in its vicinity
near the level. Their catalogue records do not establish deposition on that
floor. The metalworking hoard284 is a different deposit from nearby seal2390 at
locus235. Neither can be converted into a proven beadwork-negative seal assemblage.

The [support ledger](../results/chanhu_deposit_support.json) retains all 65
objects, seven individually reviewed named-seal associations, and explicit
unresolved status for the other 58. It establishes **no secured matched
phase/deposit/reporting stratum** in the reviewed evidence. This is not a claim
that every locus has received an exhaustive new archaeological reassessment,
that the other 58 lacked bead production, or that no broader comparison is possible.

## The weaker comparison, with its assumptions exposed

A separate [assumption record](CHANHU_LOCAL_CONTRAST_ASSUMPTIONS.md) fixed five
identities before the grouped calculation: 2285, 2428 and 2529CC in the associated
215/286/297 complex; 3215 and 3207 in the described bath442 vicinity. It uses the
previous fish audit's classifications unchanged. These are two area blocks,
one per category, with unverified comparability of deposition, exact phase and
reporting opportunity.

The fixed graphic family is P050, P051 and P056–P064 in that audit's catalogue
mapping. “Absent” below means absent under that specific rule, not absence of
every possible fish-shaped compound or proof of a shared meaning for the family.

| Described area | Certain family presence | Complete family absence | Unknown text | Presence bounds |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Associated production complex | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1/3–2/3 |
| Bath vicinity | 0 | 2 | 0 | 0 |

The conditional difference is **+1/3 to +2/3**. The plain-W220 sensitivity gives
the same result. The sole certain positive is **2428**, from passage286; 2529CC
is absent under the fixed family rule and 2285 is unknown. The five secondary
text signatures differ, but that does not establish five independent social or
depositional observations. [Exact results](../results/chanhu_local_contrast.json).

An exhaustive omission stress test over the five unsecured exact-deposit joins
has 32 subsets: 21 retain both groups and 11 have no comparison. Among available
subsets, 12 retain a strictly positive bound and nine allow zero; the combined
envelope is 0–1. Dropping the single certain positive therefore removes guaranteed
enrichment. These are sensitivity scenarios, not historical probabilities.

This supplies a transparent local association worth following up. It does not
repair the strict test, establish a bead-specific effect, provide replication
across workshops, or select among occupation, ownership, commodity, administrative
and phonetic interpretations. No p-value or translated word is reported.

## The field-record archive is now located

Penn Museum's official finding aid lists collection **1064, Chanhu-daro Pakistan
records**, including original registers and site-level notebooks in box2 and
ten object-card boxes spanning catalogued numeric ranges 1–5457. This supersedes
the earlier “archive not located” state. It does not prove completeness or the
survival/content of any individual card. The finding aid generally describes
physical reading-room material; no public card-image link was identified.
[Official finding aid](https://findingaids.library.upenn.edu/records/UPENN_MUSEUM_PU-MU.1064),
[saved archive search and limits](CHANHU_FIELDCARD_ARCHIVE_SEARCH.md).

The [15 prioritized navigation targets](../results/chanhu_fieldcard_targets.json)
identify the relevant boxes for published discrepancies and test-critical
associations, including suffix qualifications. No person was contacted and no
field-card image acquired. The archive gives a specific route to stronger
evidence; it is not itself evidence for the fish-sign reading.

All downloaded bodies and captured source responses remain together in
`data/raw/`, with byte/hash provenance in `data/sources.json`. Scripts preserve
the transcription, exact joins, uncertainty calculations and checks. Attempts,
errors and corrections are in the [research log](RESEARCH_LOG.md). The goal
remains unresolved; all 417 semantic and phonetic values remain null.

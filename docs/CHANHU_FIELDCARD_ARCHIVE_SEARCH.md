# Chanhudaro field-record archive located

28 September 2026. **An official finding aid identifies surviving catalogued field records at Penn Museum Archives**, collection **1064, Chanhu-daro Pakistan records**. Box 2 lists field notebooks, three original-register parts, two register-copy parts, room-number records and small-find/pottery tabulations. Its scope mentions site-level notebooks. Ten object-card boxes are listed as follows. [Official finding aid](https://findingaids.library.upenn.edu/records/UPENN_MUSEUM_PU-MU.1064).

The finding aid says that, unless otherwise noted, records are available in the reading room rather than digitally online. No card-image or digital-surrogate link was identified on this finding-aid page. A separate direct Penn Archives page returned a web-tool 403, so current appointment arrangements were not verified. The finding aid notes poor condition of the original register and damaged plans; it does not say the card collection was destroyed. [Collection description](https://findingaids.library.upenn.edu/records/UPENN_MUSEUM_PU-MU.1064).

The related **W. Norman Brown Papers, UPT 50 B879**, lists Mackay field reports in box 6, folders 10–12, alongside expedition correspondence. This is a supplementary lead, not the object-card series. [University Archives finding aid](https://archives.upenn.edu/collections/finding-aid/upt50b879/). MFA Boston's official access page currently states that library/archive appointments and research requests are temporarily unavailable; this search did not locate a Chanhudaro-specific MFA archival inventory. [MFA access notice](https://www.mfa.org/collections/libraries-and-archives).

## What the discovery permits

This new evidence supersedes the earlier historical “archive not located” state without altering its frozen review. It establishes a concrete catalogued holding, **not completeness, individual card survival or the contents of any card**. The printed range endpoint 5457 is not a verified card count. Nor has this task independently established that every catalogued object card is precisely one of the field cards described in the excavation preface.

As navigation candidates only, 2285 falls in box 8; 2297, 2428, 2529cc and 2558 in box 9; 3215 in box 10; 3811 in box 12; and 4852 in box 13. These placements use the numeric ranges and retain suffix uncertainty. They do not authenticate individual cards or resolve deposit discrepancies. The bounded next research target is the relevant card plus the original register and site-level notebook for the same object, compared without consulting its signs. No person was contacted, no appointment requested, and no field-record image was acquired.

# Independent verification of the frozen local-code specificity screen

This review follows the saved `CHANHU_LOCAL_SPECIFICITY_PROTOCOL.md`; new control results were inspected only after that protocol and producer output existed. The preceding design review remains unchanged. The actual protocol uses the primary 65-entry frame, a 60-object background outside the fixed five, and certain background-observed codes after excluding P050–P065 mappings, W221 and placeholders. This is narrower than the earlier all-export/all-code design proposal and must be named accurately.

`scripts/verify_chanhu_specificity.py` reads the raw CSV directly, independently scans digit spans and qualifier characters, and reconstructs all object statuses, outside-frequency sets, rational bounds, widths and profile classes. It imports neither the specificity producer nor the shared fish-status helper. It also checks that the earlier saved fish-audit rows equal the relevant raw source fields and that the producer's input hashes are current.

The reconstruction verifies 82 code rows (W220 plus 81 controls), comprising 4,920 background statuses and 410 local statuses. The background contains 60 primary objects, of which ten unjoined entries remain unknown for every candidate. W220 has two certain positives, 34 negatives and 24 unknowns there. It retains local vector `(U,P,N,N,N)` and interval `[1/3,2/3]`.

The strict outside-positive-count set has nine controls, with no equal target profile or interval. Requiring the same positive and unknown counts leaves six, again without an equal profile or interval. The wider half-to-twice band has 71 controls: W368 and W790 equal the target's **interval, but not its object-level profile**; W717 reaches the same lower bound with a different interval. None in that wider band exceeds the target lower bound. Across all 81 controls there are eight distinct local status profiles and no exact target-profile match. Outside the matched bands, W032 and W390 have strictly higher lower bounds; their background frequencies differ. The 81 codes do not constitute 81 independent patterns or replications.

The machine report additionally records which local objects support the controls meeting the target lower bound and their bounds after omitting 2428. These are explicitly post-protocol diagnostics using the already fixed five objects. W368/W790 have vector `(P,U,N,N,N)`: their certain positive is 2285, not 2428. After omitting 2428 their interval is `[1/2,1/2]`; W717, supported on 2529CC, retains `[1/2,1]`. W220 instead falls to `[0,1/2]`. Thus these observed alternative labels are not merely different codes on the sole fish-positive seal. This does not establish their meaning or archaeological association. These diagnostics do not alter the producer's control sets or target, and failure to find a strict-count tie does not authenticate a fish or gemstone reading.

## Post-hoc missing-frequency compatibility diagnostic

After the control results were known, the parent requested a marginal uncertainty check. W220's unknown texts allow a background occurrence count anywhere from 2 to 26 under the saved per-code uncertainty rule. All 81 controls have their own marginal count interval overlapping `[2,26]`. The machine report preserves each interval and its intersection. This is **necessary marginal compatibility** with equal counts, not a new matched set, a probability, or a claim that one common completion of all inscriptions realizes every equality. Shared unknown objects, sign length and coding constraints can couple completions. The diagnostic therefore weakens an interpretation of exact *observed* count matching as secure matching of actual historical prevalence; it does not invalidate or replace the declared observed-count calculation.

All results concern literal secondary W-code identities and the frozen selected family. The contemporaneous primary-photo investigation of broader fish-like shapes is separate. No broadened family or revised local status is substituted here. The unknown-count ranges, uncertain archaeological comparison, one area block per category and publication/frame selection remain material limits. No p-value or semantic/phonetic value is assigned.

The exact compared files and full verification results are pinned in `results/chanhu_local_specificity_independent.json`. No parent producer, protocol or central report was edited.

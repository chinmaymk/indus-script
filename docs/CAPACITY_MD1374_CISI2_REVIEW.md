# CISI M-1374: authenticated fragment, unestablished capacity-vessel bridge

27 September 2026. **The genuine CISI volume 2 confirms M-1374 = excavation UPM/MD-138 and supplies two photographs of a rim fragment with a seal impression. It does not supply a whole-vessel profile or establish identity with Fuls's complete vessel called MD-1374.** No new capacity, stroke value or catalogue crosswalk follows.

## Directly checked primary evidence

The retained *Corpus of Indus Seals and Inscriptions, 2: Collections in Pakistan* (1991), `data/raw/h843_identity_CorpusVol2.pdf`, has the following exact row on printed **439 / PDF 474**:

| CISI | Finnish Concordance | Excavation identifier | Museum / registration | Photograph source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M-1374 | 2893 | UPM/MD-138 | EXB 1381 | JL 346 |

The [metadata crop](../results/primary_pages/capacity-md1374-metadata-crop.png) preserves this row. The key on printed **434 / PDF 469** explicitly distinguishes the five columns: column 2 is Finnish Concordance, not an automatic Mahadevan text number; column 3 is the excavation identifier; column 4 is the holding institution and registration; column 5 identifies the photograph source. EXB is the Excavation Branch, Department of Archaeology and Museums, Government of Pakistan, Karachi. JL denotes a CISI-project photograph. The key does not expand the column-3 excavation abbreviations, so this review retains **UPM/MD-138** literally rather than treating it as a newly verified Penn Museum holding. EXB1381 is the historical published registration, not a present-day custody check.

Printed **180 / PDF 215** is headed *Mohenjo-daro 1372–1376, Seal impressions on pots*. It explicitly labels **M-1374 A(1) (50%)**, a view of a broken rim/neck fragment, and **M-1374 A(2)**, a closer view of its inscription. These are photographs, not profile drawings. The [paired crop](../results/primary_pages/capacity-md1374-photos-crop.png) includes both labels and views. The fragment has an incomplete vessel wall and no preserved base in the published view. The inscription is worn and includes a curved form and adjacent upright traces; this review does not certify a complete numbered transcription or count all faint strokes.

The two large whole-vessel photographs at the top of the same page belong to **M-1372 and M-1373**. Their labels prevent using them as M-1374's whole-vessel views. The **50%** caption is a publication reproduction ratio; the page gives no graduated metric bar, dimensions, internal section or reconstruction for M-1374. It cannot independently establish a 73cm height, 69cm maximum diameter or a usable-capacity integral for this fragment. No capacity was estimated from print scale, neighboring objects or the inscription.

## Comparison with the existing candidate and the capacity citation

The secondary lipi row **3672.1**, CISI **M-1374**, lists excavation **UPM/MD-138**, literal **+000-700-033-000+**, Mohenjo-daro, type **POT:T:s**, material Clay, condition Poor, complete N, but preservation complete. The new primary row authenticates that **CISI/excavation identifier pair**; the photo supports a fragmentary vessel. The source flags remain literal and unresolved. This does not make the damaged string a complete inscription or establish the numerical meaning of W033 or W700.

Fuls's public author-posted manuscript, section **3.3** and **Figure7(a)**, instead describes **MD-1374** as a complete vessel excavated by the University Museum, 73cm high and 69cm in maximum diameter, with painted bands and three vertical plus three slanted marks; it cites **Dales and Kenoyer1986, pp240–241**. The new public text check reconfirms that attribution but supplies no **UPM/MD-138**, **EXB1381**, **CISI M-1374**, or exact photograph/publication bridge. Its figure itself was not visually recovered in this follow-up. [Public manuscript extraction](https://www.academia.edu/8808307/VOLUMENMA%C3%9FE_VON_TONGEF%C3%84%C3%9FEN_DER_INDUSKULTUR).

The complete-vessel description and the fragment shown by CISI are a **material mismatch in the proposed join**. This is enough to reject accepting the identity from the integer1374 alone. It is not enough to prove that no historical restoration or different numbering scheme could ever relate them, nor to refute Fuls's volume calculation for his intended object. The two records stay separate. The Dales–Kenoyer profile, its exact artifact identifier and its independent scale remain the missing evidence for that capacity row.

## Why the limits matter

A W678-only search misses the two reviewed copper-tablet M138 occurrences. A W731-only search misses the photographed M334 occurrence labelled W729 on M354. The secondary transcription of the metal tang supplies W391 where the retained M77 field begins with loss. These are three different problems: incomplete category coverage, different classification boundaries and disagreement about recoverable text. A global replacement dictionary would conceal all three.

DHR-24475 demonstrates that the old native target selection is not the complete body of photographic evidence for a roof-over-forked shape. It does not rescue the previous held-out-root prediction design: the entire field has not been independently transcribed, and adding one roofed candidate does not supply the required plain/roofed comparisons across three roots. No post-hoc threshold change, additional prediction model or semantic inference was made.

The Dholavira context table printed247 row8 records24475NM, stratum6, GSQ25x10x4 and A470/D980cm. Its stage and area cells are blank. Its 'Goat' motif disagrees with the retained museum metadata's 'unicorn'. Both labels are preserved; neither is used to derive a sign value or a calendar date. For M810 the missing corner is in the animal field, illustrating why artifact fragmentation and inscription completeness must be evaluated separately.

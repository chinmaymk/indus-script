## Photograph observations and prediction disposition

H-889 A has a comb-like form at the left of the published photograph and a fish-like outline at the right. The latter has short flanking marks: the left pair is clearer, while the right marks lie in a less distinct, striated area near the edge. This is compatible with the broad M060 morphology rather than a plain unflanked fish. There is **no separately recognizable roof/caret above the fish** in this reproduction.

The fish reaches close to the upper edge, with little clear surface above its tip. Scratches, mottling and wear cross the field. Consequently, the present image supports broad unroofed compatibility but does not certify absence of every faint or damaged extra mark, precise stroke junctions, or all four flanking marks at equal confidence. The roof-state prediction is classified as **compatible, with limited negative-evidence certainty**, not an unequivocal fine-detail confirmation and not a contradiction.

The photographed outline is roughly rectangular, without an obvious large missing sector comparable to the broken K-29 field. Minor edge wear and surface loss cannot be quantified from the reproduction. Apparent completeness is not a certification that every stroke survives. No damage flag in the corpus was changed.

H-889 B displays three ring/cup-like circular forms. They are retained as the separately photographed reverse motif. They are not converted into M083, three script signs, a numeral or an inferred quantity. The two views give no extra independent artifact count.

## M315 on the already known large axe

M315 is drawn as **seven vertically stacked arches**, represented by one catalogue
code. M108 is a six-stroke group, also one code. The side's actual direction code
is 4 (top to bottom); the standardized catalogue presentation remains right to
left. Neither group is expanded into invented unit tokens.

![Both sides of 2925](../results/primary_pages/arch-extension-2925-detail.png)

The first side, retained separately for completeness, reads
`M163 M252 M261 M134 *M098 M391 M391`, with metadata 610001. Its doubtful M098
is not transferred to the clear second side. Conversely, a clear second side does
not make the complete object's first-side uncertainty disappear.

Appendix V, printed 818 / PDF 827, explicitly joins **2925 to FEM CXXVI item 5**,
the previously studied large axe. The original excavation ledger reads field
number 7535 whereas the later numerical paper uses 7835; this discrepancy remains
unresolved. Recovering the catalogue's auxiliary field identifies no additional
artifact and supplies no new mass observation. The reported 1910.030 g and its
corrosion limitations belong to the existing original-record audit. No equality
between M315 and seven instances of a semantically identified unit follows from
the graphical count.

The supporting small-axe row **2924 = FEM CXXVI item 3** was rechecked on the same
*Texts* page. It reads `M108 M342 M001 M375`, metadata **600002**, with no doubt or
loss. Code 2 records an original left-to-right direction, not a reason to reverse
the standardized string again. It shares M108 with 2925's second side while
retaining three other signs. Treating that six-stroke code as the whole small-axe
mass would require separately accounting for those signs.

## The next three codes are not repeated plain arches

![Numbered M315–M318 forms](../results/primary_pages/arch-extension-signs-315-318.png)

The numbered sign list on printed 34 / PDF 45 distinguishes the repeated arches
of M315 from more complex forms:

- **M316:** an enclosing arch with an H-like inner element.
- **M317:** an enclosing arch with five short internal strokes, arranged three
  above two.
- **M318:** a complex arched/roofed form with vertical and crossing strokes.

These descriptions are graphical. The interior strokes of M317 are not five
separate tokens, and no numeral, enclosure operator or semantic relationship is
assigned. M318 is not counted as another plain arch repeat. The old erroneous
assignment of M317 to reference 1001 is not used: the present sole M317 occurrence
is the directly checked 4032 field. No additional M317 is inferred from a visual
resemblance to M393.

## Reproduction and scope

The [structured ledger](../data/processed/arch_extension_catalogue_review.json)
contains six full line records: the four indexed fields, 2925's other side, and
supporting 2924. It preserves zero-based token indices, doubt/loss data, exact
metadata, positional counts, crop boxes and evidence fingerprints. The catalogue
fields and Table I remain separate evidence sources in the ledger.

Existing PDFs and the previously preserved excavation identity records were used;
no downloads were needed. New *Texts* renders used PDF pages 58, 65 and 109 at
scale 3500; the existing PDF 96 and concordance PDF 568 renders were re-viewed.
An initial sign-list crop clipped the right edge of M318; it was widened and the
final crop rechecked. No shared corpus, research log or sign index was edited.

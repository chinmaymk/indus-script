# The local fish-sign association is graphically supported, but not unique

28 September 2026. **The primary photograph supports a fish-like sign on the
positive seal. The small area contrast still does not uniquely select a fish
reading: a clearly preserved oval/leaf control gives identical numerical bounds
under a declared wider frequency match.** Exact-frequency controls do not tie
the target, and the competing signs occur on different objects. These
qualifications matter; this is neither a refutation nor a decipherment.

## What the photographs establish

The [positive-object review](CHANHU_LOCAL_PRIMARY_POSITIVE_REVIEW.md) verifies
CH2428 = original Plate LII.19 and CH2529CC = LII.23 by exact excavation number
and plate item. CH2428 has a certain visible plain fish-like form despite another
unclear part of its inscription. CH2529CC's displayed band has no clear member
of the fixed family. Both published views are plasticine impressions under
Mackay's stated photographic method. The crops were not mirrored or restored.
The modern C65/C69 labels remain secondary aliases in this bounded review.

The [comparator review](CHANHU_LOCAL_PRIMARY_COMPARATORS_REVIEW.md) checks
CH3215/LI.14, CH3207/LI.22 and CH2285/LI.15, including securely joined CISI1
original photographs for the latter two. Enlargements resolve the preliminary
fish-like appearance on CH3215/CH3207 into asymmetric or limb-like structures;
no new member of the fish family is established. Primary sign-list comparisons
support this distinction without assigning meanings to the forms.

CH2285's original photograph clearly loses a substantial upper corner of its
inscribed face. The older rectangular impression obscures the extent of that
loss. Its complete original text therefore remains unknown. This physically
supports the missing-text treatment; it does not identify any missing sign.

These are graphic checks, not a full independent transcription of all five
objects. In particular, a displayed impression boundary does not certify every
edge of the ancient text intact. Absence in the visible field and absence from
the entire original text remain distinct claims.

## A fixed control screen, including unfavorable results

The [protocol](CHANHU_LOCAL_SPECIFICITY_PROTOCOL.md) fixed the same five objects
and a 60-object background from the preceding 65-entry register. All ten
unjoined background objects remain unknown. Candidate controls had to occur
certainly in an accepted background text; the local outcomes did not select
them. The target is singleton W220, compared with single codes, with fish-family
and placeholder exclusions declared in advance. This tests specificity in a
secondary representation, not all possible graphic concepts or all 417 signs.

W220's background counts are **2 positive, 34 negative, 24 unknown**. The screen
contains 81 non-target controls, but only eight distinct five-object status
profiles. No p-value treats those codes as independent trials.

| Background matching rule | Controls | Equal target interval | Equal five-object profile |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Same observed positive count | 9 | None | 0 |
| Same positive and unknown counts | 6 | None | 0 |
| Half to twice the observed positive count | 71 | W368, W790 | 0 |

The exact match and wider sensitivity therefore give different answers to
whether another code ties the target. Report both. The wider band was declared
before its outcomes were calculated, not chosen after the exact match failed.
[Complete control table](../results/chanhu_local_specificity.json),
[independent reconstruction](CHANHU_LOCAL_SPECIFICITY_INDEPENDENT.md).

Using order `(2285, 2428, 2529CC; 3215, 3207)`, W220 has `(U,P,N;N,N)`;
W368 and W790 have `(P,U,N;N,N)`. Both patterns have one certain positive and
one unknown among three associated-complex seals, and no reported occurrence
among two bath-vicinity seals. Thus both yield **[1/3, 2/3]**. They tie the
grouped statistic, not the object-level observation: their certain positive is
2285, whereas W220's is 2428. This is a consequence of what grouping retains,
not proof of synonymy or shared ancient function.

W717 in the wider band has [1/3, 1]; its larger upper limit reflects more
uncertainty, not stronger evidence. Across all unmatched controls, W032 and
W390 have larger lower bounds, but neither belongs to either frequency band.
They must not be presented as frequency-matched counterexamples.

The independent review additionally notes that all 81 controls' marginal
background count intervals overlap W220's **[2,26]**. This post-protocol
diagnostic shows how uncertain true frequency matching remains. It supplies
neither a new control set nor guaranteed simultaneous historical completions.

## Checking the competing graphics and preserving uncertainty

After the control calculation, the photo reviewer examined the two tied C22
forms. The oval/leaf graphic associated with **W790** is clearly visible in the
original and impressions. Its broad crosswalk admits several M numbers; no
unique M identity or meaning is imposed. **W368** has only a damaged shaft and
wing-like remnant, insufficient to independently authenticate its full
diagnostic form. These targeted checks are explicitly post-selection.

A separate [photo sensitivity](../results/chanhu_photo_sensitivity.json) retains
all frozen classifications, but also shows the result of treating W368's local
presence as unknown: its interval becomes **[0, 2/3]**. W790's tie remains
**[1/3, 2/3]**, conditional on the other source classifications and the same
area assumptions. No source transcription is silently corrected.

The same sensitivity asks what happens if original-whole-text absence on either
bath comparison seal is withheld because complete edges are not certified by
the photographs. With either negative changed to unknown, W220's bounds become
**[−1/6, 2/3]**; withholding both gives **[−2/3, 2/3]**. These are assumptions
being tested, not claims that a missing fish existed. They show that guaranteed
positive enrichment depends on those negative classifications as well as on
the single certain positive.

## Consequence for a reading

The defensible result is a real visible fish-like form, a limited association
conditional on uncertain area/deposit and completeness assumptions, and a
nonunique grouped statistic. Exact observed-frequency matching leaves the
target distinctive among its nine controls, but the small sample, unknown
backgrounds and wider-band tie prevent that distinctiveness from identifying
“gemstone,” an occupation, a name or a phonetic value. The previously located
field-record archive remains a route to improving the external context.

The scripts save every control, missing-status bound, source pin and review
qualification. No new downloads were needed; existing sources remain together
in `data/raw/`. The [research log](RESEARCH_LOG.md) records the failed first
parser run, corrected visual leads and verification. All 417 semantic and
phonetic values remain null; the goal is active and unresolved.

# M77 U-field counterpart recovery: structure partition

The frozen partition contains 76 references, from4314 through4520. All76 are accounted for in `data/processed/m77_u_counterparts_structure.json`: **77 separate counterpart text lines**, plus **two symbol-only metadata entries**. Every selected side2/line0 stroke-plus-M328 field is excluded. Reference4386 has two separate side1 lines;4342 and4402 also have side3 symbol-only entries.

The review used the primary Texts on PDF117–129, printed106–118, and numbered sign-list pages32–35 (PDF43–46). Full-page images preserve reference numbers, metadata, neighboring rows and glyphs. Every source and render has a recursive path/SHA-256 evidence entry in the ledger. Additional300-dpi crops were used for close distinctions; they are not independent evidence or enhanced readings. The source PDF hash is `6dcdd074a0174e5fab149590fbbf82342711d345764e9763f8e5e92c81cf7f99`.

Tokens retain the book's normalized right-to-left display convention, without assigning actual artifact direction. The six metadata digits are object type, side, two field-symbol digits, line, direction. A two-digit line continuation inherits the prior side. Lines are never concatenated. `full_sequence_primary_checked` means every printed position was inspected, not that damaged or doubtful signs became certain. `all_counterpart_lines_accounted_for` means all printed entries were represented, not that lost ancient text was recovered.

Six lines contain positive printed damage (`LOSS`, unknown span length); seven contain printed doubt (`*Mddd`); two lines have both. Initial visible-glyph uncertainties were resolved by closer primary comparison, leaving no reviewer `UNKNOWN` in this partition. **66 lines** are undoubted, lossless and identified. Symbol-only metadata does not become a token sequence.

A draft was checked against the secondary candidate transcription only to flag discrepancies. The source image and numbered inventory then decided each correction. This was not blind validation and does not establish a population error rate. In particular:

- 4359/10 is M272 M402: the second glyph is a fan on a long slant, not the A-like M178 previously recorded in the pilot context.
- 4370/10 is M391 M099 M226 M254: the angular sign's upper-right terminal turns downward, unlike M216 in the earlier pilot context.
- 4410/10 contains M223 with decorated terminals, not plain M216; it must not merge with the otherwise similar4360/4499 lines.
- 4404/10 begins with two signs M293 M123, not fused M295.

The ledger records the two superseded pilot contexts and their original file hashes without overwriting them. Initial draft near-shape or numeric-label mistakes (including4345 and4373) were corrected before finalization. Three carrying-person012 assignments,042/015 at4357 and the low short stroke098 at4376 were accepted only after primary close inspection. A subsequent independent review of4479 caught a reversed morphology explanation: numbered M012 has no detached inner-leg stroke; M013 adds that stroke. The repeated explanatory notes at4353/4479/4489 were corrected after rechecking the primary sign list. Their token IDs are unchanged.

These are catalogue transcription results. Repeated counterpart strings may support a controlled comparison of graphic stroke counts, but do not alone identify numbers, commodities, personal names, spoken language, common molds or separate ancient transactions. Repeated physical production, source normalization and object-type/site dependence remain relevant to any later comparison.

Mechanical checks passed: exact76-reference partition,77 distinct side/line records, selected-field exclusion, primary sign IDs1–417 only, metadata line agreement and every referenced image hash.

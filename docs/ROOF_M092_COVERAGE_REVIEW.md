limit, not evidence that its roof was unproductive or meaningless.

A second primary depiction in the concordance, printed321/PDF332, row8018 field00,
shows the same combination and loss. This is the M099 concordance group: it is
not mislabelled as a newly recovered M092-group census. The primary TableI supplies
the one-occurrence count. No hatching is converted into an unroofed alternative,
no sides are joined, and no catalogue or ancient quantity is inferred from the
three visible strokes.

photograph. It does not establish an independently legible roof on the physical
object or authenticate every graphic variant of M089. The fixed shape review
already limits M089/M092 to representative drawings. The two catalogue depictions

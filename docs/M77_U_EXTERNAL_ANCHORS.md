# External anchors for the matched stroke-plus-U family

**No independent quantity, arithmetic equation or denomination was authenticated for the exact main `M048 M342 M176` family in this bounded review.** The primary two/three/four-long-stroke contrast remains intact. The additional archaeology supports contextual comparisons, not numerical readings. This is a result about the sources examined, not an assertion that such evidence is absent everywhere.

The target references are4548/5464 (two strokes),4509/4387 (three), and4508 (four), as authenticated in [the field review](M77_U_FIELDS.md). An external anchor would tie these exact objects, or securely matched equivalents, to independently counted goods, measured amounts, exchange values or an arithmetic relation. The visible succession2/3/4 alone supplies none of those relations; tablet dimensions are not represented amounts.

subsequently recovered the Vats photographs and individual descriptions for
4508/4509/4548 through public archive copies. It establishes dimensions, material,
shape and find information; it still does not establish represented amounts.

## Relevant archaeological comparanda

Meadow and Kenoyer report22 three-sided incised copies,16 clustered, in secondary Period3B debris outside Harappa's walls. Each face has a complex sign with three or four strokes. Microscopic review identifies at least three carvers, and the face sequence/orientation varies. A nearby seal, H96-2796/6876-01, shares its final two signs with one tablet face; associated ceramics suggest comparable dates. This is a real contextual relationship, but it supplies no independently measured amount. Their proposed link between tablet disposal and a seal owner's status, death or expiry is interpretation. The recovered totals22/16 count copies, not the amounts represented by inscriptions. These1990s finds have **not** been authenticated as the exact pre1977 target family. [Meadow and Kenoyer2000, PDF14,16–17 / offprint13,15–16](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/Kenoyer2000_The%20Tiny%20Steatite%20Seals%20of%20Harappa.pdf).

Kenoyer and Meadow's later review corrects earlier dating through re-cut stratigraphy: incised tablets appear in midPeriod3B and continue3C. It distinguishes copied incisions and molded duplicates; similar numerical-sign series can use different molds. Reported tablet deposits are secondary trash, fill or street contexts, while original uses remain unresolved. These observations do not identify a U unit or a denomination. [Kenoyer and Meadow2010, PDF/printed6–7](https://www.harappa.com/sites/default/files/pdf/KenoyerMeadow%202010%20Inscribed%20Objects%20from%20Harappa.pdf).

The previously examined vessel-capacity proposals are a separate chain: no physical association of their measured vessels with the target tablets was authenticated. A fitted unit cannot become an independent measurement through citation. See [quantity anchors](QUANTITY_ANCHORS.md) and [capacity follow-up](CAPACITY_TABLE_FOLLOWUP.md).

## Preserved evidence and limits

Its response is preserved as [raw capture](../data/raw/harp_tablet_context_web_capture.json), explicitly labeled as extracted text, not a PDF. Those online PDF pages were not visually inspected. The existing local M77 appendix images were visually inspected.

The official IGNCA Vats PDF route exceeded the web tool's22.56MB fetch limit. No broad corpus download followed. Searches for exact identifiers did not recover an authenticated goods association; this does not license conclusions about unread Vats pages, field registers, unpublished records or all literature.

[The ledger](../data/processed/m77_u_external_anchors.json) preserves URLs, attempts, exact page locators, boundaries of review and SHA-256 hashes. The parent registers the single new raw capture. Quantity and category models remain unresolved for the exact family; the new contextual evidence neither disproves counting nor establishes a value system.

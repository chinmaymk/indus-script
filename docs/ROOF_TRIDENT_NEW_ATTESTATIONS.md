# Bounded photograph review: DHR-24475 and Nausharo Ns-53

Dholavira **DHR-24475 has a recovered published photograph with an exact accession caption**. Its rightmost inscription group visibly includes a chevron above a forked vertical stem. This is a local graphic attestation of the proposed roof-over-trident form. Nausharo **Ns-53 / NS 91.02.17.01 remains photographically unresolved**. Neither result supplies a reading, a global W391=M163 crosswalk, or a new native-M corpus count.

## Dholavira identity and source

R. S. Bisht, *Excavations at Dholavira (1989–90 to 2004–2005)* (2015), printed p.286, entry 119, labels accession **24475**, notes **“In Nat.Mus”**, and places a colour **photograph, not a line drawing**, in the same row. The retained [page image](../data/raw/roof_trident_new_report_leaf302_response.bin) is Internet Archive leaf 302. The official ASI two-page cover was separately retained and visually checked for the title, author and year. The archive copy is a third-party-hosted primary report; its uploader metadata is not treated as authorship evidence. A full PDF download was stopped in favour of the exact page images.

The [stage-wise table](../data/raw/roof_trident_new_report_leaf263_response.bin), printed p.247, row 8, independently lists **24475 NM**, GSQ **25x10x4**, stratum **6**, depth/3DR **A=470cm; D=980cm**, motif **Goat**. Its inscription, area and stage cells are blank. Do not fill those blanks from adjacent rows or infer a calendar date. The retained MuseumsOfIndia metadata has exact source identifier **nat_del-DHR-24475-18639**, holding National Museum, New Delhi, but calls the object a **unicorn** seal and describes cracks on both surfaces. Preserve this **Goat/unicorn metadata disagreement**; neither animal label is needed for the inscription claim. The museum photograph endpoint itself could not be recovered.

Secondary lipi row **4196.1** explicitly names Dholavira and **DHR-24475**, with literal string **+391-002-365-550+**, type **SEAL:S**, preservation **complete**, condition **Poor**, complete **Y**. The accession, site-specific report and exact museum identifier establish the local documentary bridge, rather than similarity of the string. No translations were inspected or incorporated.

## What the photograph supports

The [native-resolution crop](../results/primary_pages/roof-trident-new-dhr24475-photo.png) shows the seal face with its inscription above the animal. At the rightmost end, two sloping strokes form a distinct roof/chevron above a lower forked sign with a long descending stem. This supports the graphic feature sought in secondary W391 at this exact object. Four apparent upper-field groups are compatible with the four-token secondary string, but **this review does not authenticate every sign ID or determine reading direction from the photograph alone**.

The four outer edges of the face are largely retained; the visible inscription is bounded by the surviving left and right margins, without an obvious missing end segment. However, the surface is abraded and cracked, and some small strokes approach the upper edge. The photograph supports **apparently retained whole-field extent with uncertain stroke-level legibility**, not a certification that every incised stroke survives. The secondary label `complete` remains a source label. No high-confidence complete transcription is promoted from it. The crop script performs rectangular cropping only: no contrast reconstruction, tracing, mirroring or redrawing.

## Attempts, preservation and reproducibility

The processed review records exact candidate metadata, findings, nulls and hashes. `roof_trident_new_download_attempts.json` records URLs and outcomes; `roof_trident_new_new_sources.json` is the pending typed registry for **every retained raw file**, including headers, metadata, tool-produced web captures, the museum 404 body, cover and report page photographs. Tool captures are expressly not original webpage/PDF bytes.

The initial downloader recorded ASI 404, Persée 403 and archive 500 exceptions without reading their response bodies; those bodies are **not retained**. A subsequent 127,352,282-byte PDF attempt was interrupted during `response.read()` after a slow transfer. Its in-memory partial response was unrecoverable and is **not represented as a retained complete or partial file**. These are preservation limitations, not successful downloads. The public page-image route subsequently recovered the exact accession photograph and context table. The museum's published HTTP port-81 URL timed out with no body; its standard HTTPS path returned a retained 404 HTML body.

`scripts/render_roof_trident_new_crops.py` recreates the three documentary crops and their manifest. No central counts, frozen primary reviews, sign crosswalks or source registry were edited. Established semantic and phonetic values remain **0**.

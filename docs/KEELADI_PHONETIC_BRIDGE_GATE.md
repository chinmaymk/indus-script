# Keeladi2019: independent phonetic bridge gate

Reviewed 11 October 2026. **This primary report supplies no qualifying independent
Indus sound correspondence.** Its Tamil-Brahmi readings and visual comparisons
are useful observations, but do not establish an aligned bilingual or transfer a
known sound into Bronze Age Indus. This does not disprove every continuity proposal.

The new pointer is Yajnadevam's compiled2024 paper, section4.16/p31 and p97
bibliography. The report was not in the retained source collection or prior named
reviews. The [frozen protocol](../data/processed/keeladi_phonetic_bridge_gate_protocol_v1.json)
allowed one exact cited original PDF URL and directly relevant internal sections;
no alternative Tamil sites, references or sign fitting. The full66-page body was
retrieved once from a public mirror. Its cover and edition page identify the Tamil
Nadu Department of Archaeology, Sivanantham/Seran,2019, publication302.
[Cited primary report](https://www.vinavu.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/Keeladi-Book-English-18-09-2019.pdf).

## Candidate claims versus independent confirmation

The candidate paper p25 Table11 labels a Keeladi reading mixed-script and gives
a600BCE date. Section4.16/p31 also proposes reversing a potsherd reading and a
stratigraphic progression from Indus to mixed inscriptions to Brahmi. We rendered
those two claim pages for comparison; the earlier candidate assessment remains
frozen, with this separate new primary-source layer.

The inspected report does not supply an independently warranted sound alignment
for those claims. Anonymous drawings or resembling strings are not an authenticated
artifact join to a known reading, marking order or layer. A grammatical reading
chosen after reversing direction cannot independently determine that direction.
Dating the site's deposits also does not, by itself, date the particular mark or
establish a Bronze Age-to-later sound correspondence.

The report itself advocates continuity. We preserve that statement rather than
portraying the report as denying it. What is missing for this gate is the evidence
assigning sounds and demonstrating the claimed sequence on individually identified
objects. No full reading disagreement is scored: a source-eligibility failure must
not be turned into a statistical rejection or a declaration that the proposed
translation is impossible.

## Result and stopping rule

Finite stop reached: relevant sections in this one report reviewed. **No qualifying
phonetic bridge admitted.** Counts1001/56 describe the report's inventories, not
independent bilingual pairs or an eligible testing denominator. Source2019 is
already cited by the candidate paper compiled2024; there is no unseen/holdout or
blindness claim. The actual fitting dates were not independently established.

No sound/meaning assignments, corpus changes, dictionary tests, p-values, external
reference expansion or outreach. Reopening requires a specifically identified
object with independent reading, context and justified mark alignment, or a new
primary document supplying the missing bridge. More shape matching alone does
not pass. The overall decipherment goal remains unresolved.

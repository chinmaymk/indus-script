# P516138: Meluhha recipients, unidentified cuneiform seal owner

Reviewed 2026-09-23. **CUSAS 40, 1354 = CDLI P516138 is verified, but supplies no readable Indus signs attached to a known owner's name.** The seal is more informative than a wholly illegible impression: CDLI transcribes a partly restored scribal title and patronymic, with the names lost. The three people in the tablet's oil allocation cannot consequently be promoted to seal owners.

The [official CDLI JSON](https://cdli.earth/artifacts/516138/json) explicitly associates P516138 with Sigrist and Ozaki, *Tablets from the Irisaĝrig Archive* (2019), CUSAS 40, exact reference 1354. This corroborates the catalogue identity of the CUSAS 40/2,1354 research lead; the export itself omits the part number. It identifies a clay administrative tablet, Sumerian, museum field `IM —`, and Irisagrig (modern location uncertain). No numeric accession, excavation number, findspot or stratum is provided. The generic HTML heading's word “excavated” therefore does not establish a controlled excavation context.

The official [ATF export](https://cdli.earth/inscriptions/2334411/atf) exactly matches the JSON's embedded ATF. Obverse 1–4 allocates one unit to `na-na-sa3`, one to `sa6-ma-ar`, and half to `a-li-a-hi dam-a-ni`; the first line's commodity/unit is partly restored. `dam-a-ni` identifies a wife in the wording following Samar; it does not establish ownership of the impression. Obverse 5 labels the group with Meluhha. The reverse supplies a royal `a-ru-a` description and mountain-goatherding role; the reading of `sipa#` is explicitly uncertain. Names, brackets and uncertainty markers remain unchanged in the ledger.

The seal section reads:

```
1. [...]
2. dub-[sar]
3. dumu [...]
```

These are catalogued cuneiform words: a partly restored scribe title and “son of” an unavailable name. No Indus sequence is transcribed. The linked P533185/S999999 is explicitly a **dummy composite for unidentified seals**, so matching that link elsewhere does not demonstrate the same physical seal.

CDLI's date is Šū-Suen.06.03.00. The available royal year formula ends in a lacuna; its preserved text alone does not independently establish year six. The month is written `iti {gesz}apin`. Keep the metadata date as reported, without supplying missing wording. Repeated recipients and allocations in Nisaba 371, CUSAS 1582 and UCTIS 099 do not alone establish one physical tablet or one event. In particular, retain this record's `na-na-sa3`, rather than silently harmonizing it to another record's `na-na-za`.

No photograph or handcopy of 1354 was obtained. The [CDLI page](https://cdli.earth/artifacts/516138) uses `/images/no-image.jpg`; its social metadata's constructed P516138.jpg URL is not proof of an image. The limited licensed book preview provides front matter and a description of the transliteration edition, but no text 1354 plate. The publisher DOI page returned an access/fetch error. No access restriction was bypassed. This audit therefore verifies catalogue testimony, not the physical glyphs, iconography or completeness of the seal impression.

The structured [review ledger](../data/processed/p516138_anchor_review.json) retains full ATF, uncertainty, access outcomes, and URL/byte/SHA-256 metadata for all five saved raw sources. JSON and separately downloaded ATF were compared exactly. No sign reading or phonetic value is inferred from these names.

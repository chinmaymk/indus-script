# Independent Vats table and context reading

28 September 2026 UTC. This review independently reads retained Vats volume I printed **254–255** (archive leaves280–281), TableI, and printed **91** (leaf117). It does not import another reviewer's transcription or assume CISI aliases. **The table securely gives heights of 3ft for1033A and2ft10in for1033B. These convert to the external heights in the retained Fuls table text, to its printed0.1cm precision.** That agreement is dimensional evidence, not a sign meaning, capacity measurement, or proof of an independent second measurement campaign.

The shaft's proposed granary function is the author's interpretation, not an established jar-content measurement.

## Row18 and its separate inscription claim

Printed254 row18 clearly reads register **3822**, MoundF, TrenchI, square **M12/6**, depth **15ft9in**, stratum **VI**. Its dimensions are **height2ft3in** and **mouth diameter11in**, or68.58cm and27.94cm under the exact2.54cm/in conversion. The description says large, napiform, in sherds. Its potsherd cell on printed255 records two small pieces, one from a jar neck; other selected content cells are dotted blanks.

The remarks explicitly describe **three pictograms at the shoulder**, note two jar stands **3823A andB** close to it, and cite **PlateXXIII,c**. These statements belong to row18/3822. They do not identify three simple tally strokes, independently verify the pictured inscription, equate3822 with1033A/B or any CISI object, or establish a numerical value. The plate itself was not inspected in this bounded review; the **table-to-plate citation** is authenticated here.

## Narrow comparison with Fuls's retained table text

The existing extracted Fuls Table1 has excavation keys1033a and1033b with external heights **91.4cm** and **86.4cm**. Vats's heights convert to **91.44cm** and **86.36cm**; rounding those to one decimal gives exactly the extracted values. The signed differences, Fuls minus converted Vats, are−0.04cm and+0.04cm. No CISI name is needed for this comparison. The nominal agreement does not establish the original measurement precision or prove that later values were independently remeasured instead of inherited from Vats.

This check does **not** corroborate the later maximum diameters85.6/83.6cm, internal heights74/85cm, opening diameters43.5/40cm, calculated volumes, or associated stroke counts. Those quantities are absent from these two Vats measurement cells. No capacity was inferred or recalculated. The fragmentary condition and mixed excavated material should remain visible in any subsequent claim about use.

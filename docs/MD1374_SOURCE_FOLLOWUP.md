# MD-1374 original-source retrieval

The original Dales and Kenoyer 1986 pages 240–241 were **not recovered** through the public routes checked on 2026-09-23. No original scaled profile or inscription was inspected. This is a bounded negative retrieval result, not evidence that the cited drawing is absent or that Fuls's calculation is wrong.

The [publisher identifies the book](https://www.penn.museum/research/publications/publication/852) as *Excavations at Mohenjo Daro, Pakistan: The Pottery*, University Museum Monograph 53, by George F. Dales and Jonathan Mark Kenoyer (1986; ISBN 9780934718523). Its page offers a purchase route rather than public full text. The [Internet Archive item](https://archive.org/details/excavationsatmoh0000dale) explicitly reports restricted access, and the [Open Library catalogue](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL2542970M/Excavations_at_Mohenjo_Daro_Pakistan) points to that same item. Restricted file endpoints were not requested.

A normal request to an indexed [distributor sample](https://www.isdistribution.com/PDF/25233210-04c9-4d3e-8d42-b51a254d1a05.pdf) returned HTTP 403; no file was written and no bypass was attempted. [Google Books page 240](https://books.google.com/books?id=4iew_THp8foC&pg=PA240) was inaccessible through the web tool; a normal browser attempt reported that no browser was available. Indexed contents place the drawings section at page 237, but do not expose the requested drawing.

The preceding [capacity followup](CAPACITY_TABLE_FOLLOWUP.md) records Fuls's public extracted text: MD-1374 is reportedly 73 cm high and 69 cm in maximum diameter, bears three vertical and three slanted marks, and has computed capacities of 140.65 L to the rim and 138.84 L below it. These are attributed reports; the capacities are geometric calculations, not liquid-fill measurements. The original profile and scale remain unverified. [Fuls source](https://www.academia.edu/8808307/VOLUMENMA%C3%9FE_VON_TONGEF%C3%84%C3%9FEN_DER_INDUSKULTUR), manuscript section 3.3 and figure 7.

A local search found a possible identifier lead: `data/raw/lipi_inscriptions.csv`, row `3672.1`, calls CISI `M-1374` a Mohenjo-daro clay pot, with excavation identifier `UPM/MD-138` and literal text `+000-700-033-000+`. This does **not** authenticate a bridge to Fuls's `MD-1374`. The integer match and object type are insufficient. Its condition is `Poor`, completeness is `N`, and the separate preservation field says `complete`; these fields are retained without reconciliation. Dimension fields are blank or zero placeholders and provide no independent dimensions. No value or shape was assigned to `700` from the proposed reading.

No downloads resulted, so there are no new raw-file hashes or source registrations. The complete attempt record and the existing CSV hash are in [the machine-readable ledger](../data/processed/md1374_source_followup.json). Until the original pages are authenticated, this vessel cannot supply an independently verified calibration row for the slanted strokes.

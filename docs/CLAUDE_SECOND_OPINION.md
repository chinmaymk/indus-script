## Critiques accepted

**The equality premise needs an operational rule.** Selecting equalities after looking at stroke ratios makes the test unfalsifiable. A future test must freeze count-independent inclusion criteria and retain every failure. In this corpus, shared context is not evidence of equal quantities. The2/3 result remains conditional algebra, with no successful independent metrological validation.

**H1923 is a conflict, not an inconvenient outlier to discard.** Under the same fixed identity and equality assumptions, its current secondary transcription contradicts the remaining equations. The no-H1923 result is a sensitivity scenario. Confirming that reading and those premises would reject the universal constant-conversion model; adding a new unit or class would be a new model.

**Duplicate groups and prior exposure matter.** Different catalogue identifiers do not establish independent observations. A held-out test must exclude all derivation objects, counterparts, known mold duplicates and previously inspected contexts. The existing export and selected550-line corpus are exploratory evidence. Rebuilding their transcription does not turn them into unseen evidence.

**Printed-source fidelity is not object accuracy.** A/B agreement validates the transcription process relative to M77, not Mahadevan's underlying readings or sign classification. Future object-photo checks should sample agreed rows as well as disagreements; the current batch offers no independent population error rate.

**A substitution null model may be testable before numerical meaning is.** Predeclared minimal-pair counts and permutations can test whether a particular stroke exchange is overrepresented. Marginal frequencies, context, site and duplicates must be controlled. Even a positive result would not establish equal value.

## Claims not adopted without evidence

The response broadly asserted that Indus seals/tablets have no totals or summations. This tools-disabled review did not establish that universal negative; it is not added as a corpus fact. Its suggestion that balanced examples might share a mold remains a possible dependence, not a verified identity. Chance-corrected agreement was suggested, but cannot be manufactured from this nonrandom, partially exposed page.

The comparative proto-cuneiform note remains useful methodological evidence: commodity-specific numerical systems existed, and two fraction pairs have ratio2/3. It adds no likelihood or sign mapping to the Indus hypothesis. Claude was not asked to approve a decipherment, and none is claimed.

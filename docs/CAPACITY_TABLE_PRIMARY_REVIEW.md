# Fuls capacity tables: bounded primary-source recovery

**The original Table 1 and Table 2 images remain unrecovered.** This follow-up obtained legitimate publisher front matter, which confirms the article's location but cannot authenticate its numerical cells or resolve the H-370/H-372 excavation-number conflict. The earlier public-text transcriptions remain provisional.

The [publisher book page](https://shaker.de/de/site/content/shop/index.asp?lang=de&ID=8&ISBN=978-3-8440-0875-3) links a full document, free contents and a free abstract. The full-document link redirects to a login page. The two free links lead through public HTML download wrappers to PDFs; those exact supplied links were followed. No purchase, credential entry or access-control workaround was attempted.

The retained [six-page contents PDF](../data/raw/capacity-table-primary-shaker-contents-document.pdf), PDF page 6, visibly lists Andreas Fuls and the target article starting on page 101. The [page image](../data/raw/capacity-table-primary-contents-page6.png) preserves that check. The [three-page abstract PDF](../data/raw/capacity-table-primary-shaker-abstract-document.pdf) contains a proceedings-level description, visible on [PDF page 3](../data/raw/capacity-table-primary-abstract-page3.png). Extraction of all nine pages and visual review of these two pages found no target tables. The contents do not authenticate the article's final page or justify transferring manuscript page offsets into the published volume.

The [author's bibliography](https://www.epigraphica.de/indus/menueindus.htm) repeats the 2012 citation and pages 101–114, but supplies no article PDF link there. Exact-title, author, ISBN, publisher and institutional-domain searches did not locate another legitimate public copy within this bounded search. Nonprimary and unrelated search results were not used. A normal fetch of the author-posted Academia landing page returned HTTP 403; the complete error body and headers were retained. The previously blocked attachment download endpoint was not retried.

The earlier ledger reports CISI assigning `12908` to H-370 and leaving H-372 unknown, whereas the Fuls public-text table assigns `12908` to H-372 and `1033b` to H-370. This follow-up adds no image evidence that resolves that conflict. It also does not place the missing H-372 dimensional cell, authenticate K-90's scale, or upgrade modeled capacities into direct measurements. The existing ledger and calculations were left unchanged.

The [review ledger](../data/processed/capacity_table_primary_review.json) records search queries, nine fetch attempts, source pins, technical failures and limits. The [pending registry](../data/processed/capacity_table_primary_downloads.json) lists all **18** new raw artifacts with bytes, SHA-256, URL and type: response bodies, HTTP headers, two actual PDFs and two local page renders. Every raw artifact uses the `capacity-table-primary-` prefix in `data/raw/`. Two files with `.pdf` names are actually the publisher's HTML download wrappers; their registry types say so. The `.partial` Academia file is a complete error response, not a partial article.

A licensed or author-supplied copy remains the concrete next source requirement. This negative recovery result concerns access within the attempted routes; it is not evidence against the existence of the tables or any ancient metrological hypothesis.

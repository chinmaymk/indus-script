# Vessel measurements: confirmed heights, unresolved numerical interpretation

The new primary pages confirm the recorded heights of excavation jars **1033A and
1033B**, but do not provide an independent capacity calibration. The two jars came
from one pavement/drain setting. Catalogue conflicts and uncertainty about how
strokes form signs prevent treating the earlier three-vessel fit as a securely
identified validation set. No new sign meaning or sound follows.

## Measurements and context recovered

Vats, *Excavations at Harappa* (1940), volume I, printed91, describes two napiform
jars beside a broken pavement and drain in MoundF, squareM11/16. B lay south under
the drain; A was west, half buried, with its upper part above the pavement.
Footnote2 directs the reader to the contents table on printed254–255. These are
explicit field-number links, independent of matching marks or numerical fits.
The nearby shaft dimensions on printed91 describe the shaft, not either jar.

| Field number | Table I row | Recorded height | Converted height | Fuls public-text external height |
|---|---:|---|---:|---:|
| 1033A | 2 | 3ft | 91.44cm | 91.4cm |
| 1033B | 3 | 2ft10in | 86.36cm | 86.4cm |

Both rows give F/I/M11/16, depth5ft6in, stratumIII. Row2 describes a large napiform
jar; row3 has a ditto in that column. Vats labels the dimension simply height;
he does not distinguish external and internal height in these cells. The
converted numbers round to the later reported external heights. The conversion
uses 2.54cm/inch; the precision of the original measurements is not known to
hundredths of a centimetre. Agreement does not prove a second independent
measurement campaign: shared archival provenance remains possible.

PlateXXIII(a) explicitly identifies 1033A/B beside the broken pavement of
stratumIII. It gives no usable graduated metric scale or legible independent
stroke count. Its figures and people were not used as measuring standards.

The continuation table records a seated male toy, miscellaneous sherds, a round
faience bead and charcoal for1033A. For1033B it records cake fragments, goblet/vase
sherds, bangle fragments, and charred wheat and slag. The table also reports
animal/bird bones. Its remarks describe both jars as fragmentary beside the
pavement. These are deposition assemblages; they do not establish what a mark
originally denoted, the jar's first use, or how much it held. Vats's chapter title
uses a post-cremation interpretation; that historical interpretation has not
been independently established here. In particular, 1033B's wheat cannot be
assigned to a CISI sign sequence through the unresolved H370 alias.

## Corrections to the earlier candidate fit

The [fresh CISI review](CAPACITY_H370_IDENTITIES.md) confirms **H370=12908,
H371=1033a, H372=unknown**. The public Fuls table text instead pairs H372 with12908
and H370 with1033b. The measured-height agreement cannot choose which catalogue
association is mistaken. H371/1033a is the agreed link. The column containing
4607–4609 is **Finnish Concordance**, not automatically Mahadevan. Earlier
`mahadevan_ref` fields in the frozen capacity review are unverified and must not
be used as such a crosswalk.

The photographs also distinguish primitive marks from a numeral interpretation.
H371 has seven upright traces and an oblique trace; one upright plus the oblique
may belong to one angular sign, leaving six tallies. H370 has seven long traces
and a faint short candidate that could be incidental. H372 has three clear left
uprights and additional angular/short marks. The earlier 7/6/3 numerical
segmentation remains a hypothesis, not three secure numerical observations.
No count was selected to improve a capacity fit.

The [CISI2 review of M1374](CAPACITY_MD1374_CISI2_REVIEW.md) recovers a rim fragment
with a seal impression, excavationUPM/MD-138. It does not establish identity with
Fuls's complete 73×69cm vessel called MD1374. The number alone cannot join these
records. This does not refute a calculation about Fuls's intended object.

The [publisher-source search](CAPACITY_TABLE_PRIMARY_REVIEW.md) recovered the
contents and abstract, but no Table1/2 page images. The table alignment and
identifier conflicts therefore remain open. Prior numerical fits are retained
as reproducible conditional calculations; their arithmetic is not promoted to
physical or semantic validation. This report qualifies the earlier
[capacity report](CAPACITY_MODELS.md) without silently rewriting its frozen inputs.

## What could be tested next

TableI row18 also identifies **3822**, F/I/M12/6, stratumVI, with height2ft3in
(68.58cm) and mouth diameter11in (27.94cm); the continuation says it has three
pictograms at the shoulder. This is an explicit inscribed-vessel measurement
lead, but is not a fourth rim-tally capacity datum. We have neither an internal
profile/fill measurement nor an authenticated tally interpretation for it.
The [two table regions](../results/primary_pages/capacity-context-table-row18-left.png)
and [continuation](../results/primary_pages/capacity-context-table-row18-right.png)
preserve that distinction. The discovery is logged before any test using it.

No new complete measurement-and-count pair satisfies the previously stated
[prospective test design](DECIPHERMENT_EVIDENCE_GAP_REVIEW.md). That design still
requires a finite independently selected vessel class, secure artifact joins,
masked profile and sign reviews, uncertainty bounds, and comparison against a
vessel-class baseline. Two jars from one recorded setting should share a context
group if their aliases are eventually resolved; their find association does not
by itself prove common manufacture.

The [measurement script](../scripts/analyze_capacity_context.py) reproduces the
unit conversions and preserves the unaccepted joins in
[structured results](../results/capacity_context_measurements.json).
An [independent page review](CAPACITY_CONTEXT_INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md) checks the
split-table readings. All downloaded bodies, errors and headers are retained in
`data/raw/`, with typed records and hashes in `data/sources.json`. There are zero
new measured fill volumes, zero new complete external test pairs, and zero
assigned semantic or phonetic values. The overall decipherment goal remains open.

# Register7856: a jar record and a goblet reference must remain distinct

Vats1940 volumeI TableI, printed258–259, serial44 has register **7856**,
MoundF, TrenchI, square **M12/7 and8**, depth6ft, stratumIII. The containing jar
is described as large, round and cracked. Its measurement cell is blank. The
contents include one small tumbler and two miniature lids, various sherds and
other objects. The remarks specifically distinguish **two goblet sherds: one
incised and another bearing a seal impression**. No separate register or plate
number is supplied for either sherd in the table. Cereal is reported in porous
earth in the lower part; it does not identify the sherds' original use.

The [left-hand row](../results/primary_pages/jar44-table-left.png) and
[right-hand remark](../results/primary_pages/inscribed-frame-independent-p259-row44.png)
preserve the serial linkage. Dotted blank goblet and measurement cells are not
zero counts or dimensions. The marks belong to contained sherds; the table does
not report an inscription on the containing jar.

The [original narrative on printed91](../results/primary_pages/jar44-narrative91.png)
also uses7856, but describes a **fragment of an inscribed goblet**, in squareM12/7,
and compares it with No73 in PlateCIV. The original numeral is readable even
though retained OCR mangles it and an exact7856 text search fails. This is a
positive documentary co-reference with overlapping location and differing
object descriptions. It could reflect an assemblage register, loose reference,
or an error; this review does not choose among those explanations.

In particular, neither the shared number nor the comparison establishes which
of the table's two marked sherds is meant. “Similar to” does not authenticate the
illustrated No73 as this very sherd or prove use of the same stamp. The whole
jar, the two contained fragments and the illustrated comparison are retained as
separate nodes. No text is copied between them.

An exact substring search in the retained secondary excavation field returns
two faces of **DK7856**, a Mohenjo-daro implement, CISIM2118. Those are a different
site, identifier prefix and object class, and are rejected. Their sign strings
are preserved only as excluded search output in the
[structured review](../data/processed/jar44_contained_goblets_review.json).
That bounded search does not prove the Harappa object absent from other sources.

The [reproducer](../scripts/persist_jar44_review.py) saves two exact crops,
the field-specific candidate search and the distinct source relations. No
capacity, numerical interpretation, sign identity or semantic value is assigned.

# Kalibangan pipes and the proposed bead-string signs

The pipes establish that fish-like and chain-like signs were incised on fitted
terracotta pipe sections. The inspected excavation reports do not independently
establish bead contents or bead-storage use. The published seal examples are
real co-occurrences, but their selected frequency does not demonstrate a special
fish/bead relationship in a comparison matched by site, object type and text
length. Neither observation supplies a translation key.

## The physical objects and the missing contents anchor

The [original-context review](KALIBANGAN_PIPE_CONTEXT_REVIEW.md) checks the
Kalibangan reports against the [2025 bulletin](../data/raw/irc_2025_fish_bulletin.pdf),
sections4.2 and7. PartI p74 says the pipes were not in situ. PartI p556 describes
two wheel-made, well-fired pieces from a late level in square ZA8 of KLB-1.
It assigns the fish to registration21582 and three connected ovals to21583,
comparing the latter with Mahadevan415/417. PartII p1060 places them in a
residential context, ZA8 quadrant3, PhaseV. The illustrated sections fit and
have a reported joined length of56cm.

The descriptions do not explicitly say the pair was found joined. Their
photographed fit establishes mechanical compatibility; it does not independently
establish a single ancient two-sign inscription or bead-container use. No recovered
beads, diagnostic residues, closures or storage-specific wear are reported for
these objects in the reviewed entries. Absence from these entries is a limitation
of the available evidence, not proof that such features never existed.

Nor does “not in situ” select storage from other possibilities, including displaced,
unused or reused pipe components. Incision is visible, but the pipe entries do
not establish whether it occurred before or after firing. The adjacent pottery
section distinguishes these timings; that classification cannot simply be
transferred to the pipes. The bulletin's suggested contents and generated images
are hypotheses and illustrations, not observations of ancient contents.

The secondary export calls the fish pipe K-447 and assigns registration21583,
contradicting the PartI prose assignment. Its chain entry K-446 has no excavation
number. Preserve the conflict; do not silently exchange registrations or certify
a missing exact alias. The three-loop graphic family bridges provisionally to
W215/W216 and M415/M417, but does not make every loop variant identical.

## What the published comparison actually counts

The [independent figure review](PIPE_CHAIN_FIGURE_REVIEW.md) identifies13 displayed
rows but12 distinct CISI labels in Figure7A: M-324 appears twice. The export has
two identical rows on that two-sided object. Thirteen inscription rows therefore
must not be described as thirteen independent physical seals.

The [audit output](../results/pipe_chain_audit.json) finds15 W215/W216-bearing
rows across14 secondary object groups:13 seal rows, the chain pipe, and pottery
M-1599. Nine seal rows preserve more than two certain tokens. The four selected
fish-or-U-sign cases match the paper's named examples:

| Case | Certain candidate present | Important qualification |
|---|---|---|
| M-46 | Fish, including W220 | Complete export; multiple fish variants |
| M-636 | Fish, including W220 | Partly damaged, unknown token, incomplete |
| M-1765 | W705 U-shaped form | “Gold” remains the author's proposed meaning |
| Krs-1 | W706 U-shaped form | Same qualification; different exact code |

H-656 has two surviving signs and an uncertain original length, so its apparent
shortness cannot prove it had no opportunity to contain a third sign. M-1783's
missing text also prevents treating an unobserved target as a secure negative.
The audit retains unknowns explicitly. Surviving positive signs can still be
counted in damaged inscriptions.

## Matched comparison and its limits

The [protocol](../data/processed/pipes_protocol.json) and
[script](../scripts/audit_pipe_chain.py) define an exploratory comparison using
single-row objects from the existing strict subset, complete preservation,
seal types and lengths above two. Exact strata are site, literal object type and
token length. This leaves931 secondary object records, including six chain-bearing records.
The paper's damaged M-636 is excluded from this comparison, so its numerator
must not be carried over unchanged. M-1783 is excluded for uncertain text and
M-99 for its slightly chipped preservation; those exclusions remain visible in
the raw evidence inventory.

| Target | Observed among6 | Conditional expectation | Descriptive upper tail |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Selected fish family | 1 | 2.749 | .989 |
| Plain W220 only | 1 | .837 | .620 |
| W705/W706 | 2 | 1.928 | .652 |
| Fish family or W705/W706 | 3 | 4.031 | .946 |

The script convolves exact fixed-margin hypergeometric distributions across
strata. Khirsara's stratum has only Krs-1, so its positive is fixed and provides
no within-stratum comparison. At Mohenjodaro, the broad union is present in
18/25 complete eight-sign square seals and17/18 nine-sign square seals. Its
presence on M-46 and M-1765 is therefore not unusual under these matched margins.
Deduplicating identical complete strings within site/type leaves918 text types
and gives nearly identical results; the union upper tail is .946.

These are descriptive calibrations of a previously selected pattern, not
confirmatory significance tests or proof of no association. There are only six
eligible chain cases; selection, transcription, grammatical position, chronology
and excavation context remain uncontrolled. Exchangeability within these strata
is an explicit comparison assumption. The test cannot distinguish linguistic
from nonlinguistic notation or identify a commodity.

## Corrections and result

An initial common-glyph guess incorrectly identified W480 as the author's
gold-like candidate. Independent figure review corrected this to W705/W706.
The [rejected probe](../results/pipe_chain_rejected_W480_probe.json) and initial
protocol are retained, and the change is explicitly retrospective. The striking
W480 statistic from that probe is not evidence for the paper's gold reading.
The final comparison uses the pictured U-shaped candidates only; it does not
silently expand them to every member of a graphic family.

The positive evidence is an authentic pair of inscribed, compatible pipe sections
and a reproducible set of local sign co-occurrences. The crucial independent
contents-to-sign link is missing. Inferring bead storage from presumed sign
meanings and then using that storage function to confirm the same meanings is
circular. Keep bead-storage and bead-sign interpretations as unverified candidates;
no semantic or phonetic value is established for any of the417 signs.

# Bahrain three-name tablet: source audit

Gelb's primary published reading confirms a three-person name list with a
father-son relation. It does not establish the owner of an adjacent Indus seal
or give any Indus sign a name or sound.

## Published reading and its limits

[Ignace J. Gelb, *Computer-Aided Analysis of Amorite* (1980), AS21](https://isac.uchicago.edu/publications/computer-aided-analysis-amorite),
printed2/PDF18, states that his evidence was a rough copy brought from Denmark
by Juris Zarins, rather than an autopsy of the tablet. The published page was
rendered and visually checked. It identifies the Danish excavation at Qalʿat
in Bahrain, proposes a post-UrIII or Isin-Larsa date, and reads a list of three
people, the third son of the first. The visible transliteration is:

| Printed item | Transliteration in Gelb | Role in the published reading |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `1 Ia-a-bi-na-im` | First listed person |
| 2 | `1 DINGIR-mi-il-kum` | Second listed person |
| Reverse3 | `1 I-zi-ta-am-bu` | Third listed person |
| 4 | `DUMU I-a-bi-na-im` | Third person's father, identified with the first |

The printed first name begins `Ia-a-`, whereas the patronymic has `I-a-`.
This difference is retained rather than silently harmonized. Gelb nevertheless
normalizes both as the same name, Janbiʾ-naʿim. The other normalized names are
ʾIla-milkum and Jiṣiʾ-tambu. These are reported readings, not a new cuneiform
collation. The ancient tablet itself and its copy have not been examined here.
The page provides no tablet excavation number, museum accession, seal number,
relative find distance, or seal impression correspondence.

## Later edition and excavation leads

Potts's own later account, *The Arabian Gulf in Antiquity*1 (1990), printed219,
note155, points to **R.H.Brunswig Jr., A.Parpola and D.T.Potts, “New Indus Type
and Related Seals from the Near East,” BBVO2 (1983), p107, plate3 fig12** for a
copy, transliteration and translation. That original edition remains
unrecovered. The later account was accessible as a web transcription, not as
an inspected printed page. It places the tablet north of the North Wall
Sounding and repeats the three names and patronymic.

Potts printed197 note31 cites Bibby1986p114 for association with an inscribed
Harappan seal and pottery fitting the first three CityII levels, and
Højlund1986p223 for CityIIA pottery. Printed204 proposes that the tablet was no
later than level19. These are attributed chronological inferences, not an
independently recovered find register. [Accessible transcription](https://dokumen.pub/the-arabian-gulf-in-antiquity-vol-1-9780198143901.html).

The original excavation volume *Qala'at al-Bahrain*1 (1994), figure1715,
remains a retrieval target. The author's public ResearchGate upload is listed,
but its download failed in this audit. No figure number is promoted to an
excavation or museum identifier.

An indexed *Sind Quarterly*11(1),1983p53 passage reports about half a meter
between tablet and seal. Its relative-depth wording is corrupted in the search
extraction. Both this PDF and *Sind through the Centuries*1981 returned403;
the unit cannot be recovered securely. **Neither “three cm” nor “three
hundred” is accepted as a verified depth.** These papers may repeat the same
report and do not establish independent observations.

## Rejected joins and result

Læssøe's1957 *En kileskrift fra Bahrain* concerns a capacity-marked potsherd
from level23. Bibby's1964 *Arabiens arkæologi* describes a tablet fragment in a
Kassite building discovered1961/62. Their publisher abstracts identify
different artifacts; neither supplies the missing three-name tablet identity.

A list of names near a seal is compatible with several people having used,
owned or handled it. Proximity alone does not select one of the three people,
prove that the inscription spells a name, or turn the objects into a bilingual.
No name alignment was attempted. Exact accession, excavation identifier,
verified distance and depth, original tablet image and original1983 edition
remain outstanding.

## Retained evidence and attempts

All retrieved source files are in `data/raw/`; proposed registry entries are
in `data/processed/bahrain_context_downloads.json`. The review ledger pins the
Gelb PDF, publisher HTML, bounded web capture and viewed page. A saved text
extract of printed2 supports navigation, while the page image governs the
transliteration. Existing `scripts/inspect_research_pdf.py` reproduces rendering.

The bounded searches covered title/author variants of the1983 chapter, BBVO2,
the Qala'at excavation volume, the names and figure1715. They recovered
bibliographic references but not the1983 chapter. ResearchGate403, SindPDF403,
and two obsolete ISAC PDF URLs returning404 are recorded in the ledger. The
current ISAC publisher page supplied a working versioned S3 PDF link.

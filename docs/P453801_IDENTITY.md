# P453801 identity and publication audit

Reviewed23 September2026. **P453801 is Nisaba15/2,0371; P516366 is CUSAS40/2,1582. They are distinct catalogue records, but their physical independence is unresolved.** BDTNS explicitly reports a probable same-text identification and a conflicting year name. Do not merge them silently or count them as independent witnesses.

CDLI's current P453801 record was successfully retrieved after earlier failures. It lists museum label **Anonymous453801**, BDTNS192941, no displayed image, and no excavation number or accession. This is not a recoverable named museum accession. Its date is Šū-Suen06.12.00. Obverse2 now reads `na-na-sa3`/Nanasa; revision2249158 had `na-na-za`/Nanaza. The history attributes that change to25 June2017, entered by Robert Englund with credit to Tohru Ozaki. The illegible seal entry remains. [Current catalogue](https://cdli.earth/artifacts/453801), Text, Chronology and Collections; [history](https://cdli.earth/artifacts/453801/history/atf); [2015 revision](https://cdli.earth/inscriptions/2249158).

The original publication is David I. Owen2013, *Cuneiform Texts Primarily from Iri-Saĝrig / Āl-Šarrākī and the History of the Ur III Period*, Nisaba15, CDL Press, Bethesda. PartI is Commentary and Indexes; PartII is Catalogue and Texts. The primary catalogue links P453801 to **text371**, not page371. An original page/plate and its image were not verified in this metadata-only subtask. [CDLI publication110334](https://cdli.earth/publications/110334), bibliographic note and related-artifact row371.

P516366 currently identifies CUSAS40,1582, published by Marcel Sigrist and Tohru Ozaki2019, *Tablets from the Irisaĝrig Archive*, PartsOne/Two. Its museum field is **IM—**, not a complete accession. It shares the three named recipients, ration amounts and an illegible seal entry with P453801, but the reverse year formula has different damage indications and wording. Both records link P533185/S999999, explicitly a **dummy composite for unidentified seals**. That shared placeholder does not demonstrate a common seal matrix, owner or impression. [P516366 catalogue](https://cdli.earth/artifacts/516366), Text, Seals and References.

BDTNS192941 supplies a materially different account: anonymous private collection, Paris, France; date **SS08-12-00**; reverse year formula includes `ma2-gur8 mah`, whereas CDLI uses `na-ru2-a-mah`. M. Molina's remark attributes a probable same-text identification of CUSAS40/2,1582 and Nisaba15/2,0371 to Sigrist/Ozaki while retaining the year-name discrepancy. This is a documented catalogue hypothesis, not a settled artifact join. [BDTNS192941](https://bdtns.cesga.es/catalogo_directo_new.php?numBDTS=192941), Date, Collection, Remarks and reverse4–8.

For analysis, retain both source IDs and both date/name readings, with an unresolved possible-duplicate group. Name spelling changes cannot be taken as ancient allography without examining the underlying cuneiform. No seal-image interpretation was attempted here. The useful next step is publication/image collation of text371 against text1582, especially reverse year signs and physical damage; the parent and iconography agent handle that separate work.

The [ledger](../data/processed/p453801_identity_review.json) lists exact raw paths, URLs, hashes and limitations. New acquisitions here are P516366 HTML/JSON/current ATF plus a web-response capture. The P453801 downloads and BDTNS HTML were acquired by the parent/other agent and reused. No external communications, purchases or large scans were made.

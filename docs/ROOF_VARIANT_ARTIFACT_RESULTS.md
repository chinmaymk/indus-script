# Roof candidates: variant drawings and original artifacts

27 September 2026. **The roof feature is attested on original artifacts, but its shared function remains unestablished.** Published variants also show that the underlying drawings are not geometrically fixed. These findings narrow the hypothesis without supplying a translation.

This follow-up keeps the six candidate pairs and the earlier context screen frozen. It adds targeted Appendix I drawings and object-level evidence. Selection was informed by the earlier results; this is not an independent statistical holdout.

The six Appendix rows contain **15 drawings**, not 15 independently identified artifacts. Their selection and grouping come from Mahadevan's catalogue. They cannot independently prove that variants were ancient allographs. Likewise, repeated photographs of K-29 are evidence about one seal, not several attestations.

The distinction between manufactured shape and damage matters. Vats explicitly describes EH407 as entire and explains the cut-in waist of this tablet class. K-29, in contrast, has a large physical break through the inscription field. A notched outline alone is therefore insufficient to label an inscription incomplete. This does not settle the preservation of the unidentified 5444 object.

## What remains testable

The strict hypothesis “every member is the same fixed base drawing with one detached roof added” is too strong for these drawings. A looser hypothesis—an ancient roof feature having a common function across variable bases—remains possible. Neither graphic recurrence nor failure to obtain a context rectangle establishes that function.

The [frozen context test](ROOF_MODIFIER_RESULTS.md) still has zero qualifying cross-root rectangles in 1,293 strict and 2,056 broader complete-field records. Original photographs authenticate two rare roofed forms but add no missing complete rectangle corner. No new null significance test is warranted by these image checks.

For a next occurrence-level test, distinguish visible roof status, base form, damage and object identity before examining sequence effects. The most immediate unresolved source join is 5444 to Pu35:3863 or a modern catalogue entry retaining 3027(2). Even that join would establish provenance, not modifier semantics. A functional claim needs independently identified complete objects with matched contexts across graphic roots, followed by an external check capable of distinguishing the proposed meaning from rival meanings.

The K-29 check also supports **local W044/M092 correspondence** through exact object identity and catalogue feature P149. It does not validate a global conversion, the whole W transcription, the meaning of the missing material, or the equation of the added roof with standalone M134. No semantic or phonetic value is assigned to any of the 417 signs.

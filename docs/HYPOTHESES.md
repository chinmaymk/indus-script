# Hypotheses and discriminating tests

These are exploratory conclusions formed after examining the present data. This
document is a prospective specification for future data, not a claim of preregistration.
“Compatible” is weaker than “confirmed.”

The [L-51 ending audit](L51_MOTIF_AUDIT.md) records a positive structural constraint:
eight of nine eligible complete W527–W555 ending cases contain modified fish,
above an exact site/type/length matched expectation of4.16. The same-terminal
comparison cannot isolate W555 because its active strata contain almost no
varying controls. The motif remains unidentified; this association supplies no
independent gemstone, guild, tax or phonetic anchor. Future hypotheses should
explain the specific modified forms without silently merging them with plain fish.

The [Kalibangan pipe audit](PIPE_CHAIN_AUDIT.md) supplies an authentic marked-object
pair, but no independently identified contents. Fish/chain marks cannot both infer
bead storage and receive their semantic validation from that inferred storage.
The four named fish/U-sign co-occurrences are reproduced, with duplication and
damage distinguished. Matched complete-object comparisons supply no enrichment
evidence for the selected union; this neither proves absence of an association
nor rejects bead semantics. Original field joins, closures, residues or independently
identified contents would make a stronger discriminating test.

The [Umma anchor search](UMMA_ANCHOR_AUDIT.md) finds no authenticated join
between either reviewed Indus object and a readable cuneiform inscription.
The older sealing supports cloth contact, not a cloth/commodity reading. The
modern primary object page is still missing. Shared site names, nearby tablets
and similar packaging must not be promoted to translations without independent
object identity and textual correspondence evidence.

The [patronymic test](PATRONYMIC_TEST_DESIGN.md) considers a literal internal
relation marker in `A D B` / `B D C`. The M001 human-shaped candidate yields
no exact chain in five domestic parses; seven eligible foreign strings contain
no M001. This leaves other patronymic constructions and sign variants untested.
Ten matches through six alternative delimiters show why a positive chain alone
cannot identify genealogy. Matching endpoints under a fixed-marginal shuffle
also has a mathematically invariant walk count and is not a useful significance
test of this proposal.

The [primary-table extension](PRIMARY_INVENTORY.md) adds all-sign constraints without
a provisional crosswalk: M342 has 421 initial/medial counteroccurrences to a strict
line-end-only rule; modified jar forms and tall/short two-stroke signs have strongly
different positional profiles. These are constraints to explain, not established readings.

The [context-bound extension](CONTEXT_BOUNDS.md) shows that differing site mixtures
with identical within-site empirical position rates cannot reproduce any of the
five selected shape contrasts. Separate object-type mixtures are also insufficient
for the fish and stroke comparisons. Joint context, chronology and contextual
allography remain unresolved; this is an exact marginal constraint, not a semantic
identification or a population-level significance test.

The function of a text and the mechanism used to encode it are separate axes.
Administrative, identifying, ritual, and linguistic uses can coexist. A mixed-function
corpus does not license a different ad hoc translation rule for every inscription.

The [physical Lothal sealing study](SEALING_FUNCTION_ANCHORS.md) supports closure
and storage contexts for selected inscribed stamps. It does not identify the goods.
A recurrent stamp crosses proposed container classes, restricting an exclusive
container-label reading under those identifications. Preserve the distinction
between physical traces, reconstructed containers, unknown contents and proposed
text meanings. The table's W1 findspots are inferred from recurrent stamps and
cannot independently validate stamp-to-warehouse association.

The [metal-tool convention audit](METAL_NUMERAL_IDENTIFIABILITY.md) adds a precise
limit to the numeral hypothesis: the nine proposed count expressions admit every
uniform integer base≥8 under canonical carrying, as well as mixed ratios. Before
testing further tool masses, a candidate must fix which signs belong to the number,
whether carrying is required, which objects share units, whether signs encode
surviving or original mass, and independently defensible bounds on loss. Allowing
an arbitrary loss for each object guarantees a fit and cannot identify a base.
The recovered [drawings](METAL_PLATE_AUDIT.md) improve source control but supply
none of those semantic premises. New research should prioritize an independent
quantity/value association over further fits to the same five masses.

The [primary U-field comparison](M77_U_FIELDS.md) strengthens the quantity-like
candidate: identical main text occurs with two, three and four long strokes
followed by U on miniature tablets with matching original direction codes.
It contradicts a deterministic mapping from main string alone to auxiliary
graphic count. It does not distinguish quantities from category labels or
conditioned variants. U also occurs outside this simple field, including solus
and before non-stroke signs. No universal quantity reading is assigned.

## Prospective acceptance criteria for a candidate key

- **Coverage denominator:** account separately for every M sign, every observed W
  sign, tokens, objects, and damaged/unread cases. State mapping uncertainty.
- **Frozen rules:** specify sign values, modifiers, direction, word segmentation,
  allographs, homophones, and allowed exceptions before opening a new test set.
- **Independent anchors:** identify the archaeological or comparative evidence fixing
  each proposed semantic/phonetic value. Distributional similarity is not an anchor.
- **Blind prediction:** predict a previously unexamined inscription or its independently
  known context. Compare to alternative languages and structured nonlinguistic codes.
- **Failure accounting:** retain every counterexample. Do not delete rare signs or
  introduce silent values simply to rescue a preferred reading.
- **External verification:** check proposed numbers/units against measurements, proposed
  names against independent attestations, and proposed morphology against historical
  linguistic constraints. A self-consistent parser alone is insufficient.

No credible key has been produced here that meets these criteria for all 417 images.
Until anchors are supplied, the correct per-sign value is an explicit unknown with
constraints, rather than a convenient word assignment.

The [relation-template extension](RELATION_TEMPLATE_RESULTS.md) covers prefix,
internal and suffix placements for all 417 individual codes. M001 yields no exact
chain even after all 131 cuts of its 36 eligible suffix strings are allowed.
This does not refute kinship, and other codes' positive overlaps do not establish
it. A future family interpretation must authenticate the word boundaries and
person identities independently; pairwise matches may require incompatible cuts
in the same middle inscription.

## Parallel discriminating tests

The [parallel comparison](PARALLEL_THEORIES.md) tests three narrower extensions:
universal transfer of copper motif labels, fixed class ordering within every
adjacent tally run, and additional sequential context beyond adjacent signs.
The first two encounter explicit source-level counterexamples. The third yields
a small, smoothing-dependent short-context gain and inconclusive first-sign memory
after a calibration check. These outcomes do not exclude generic animal words,
order-free addition, multiple adjacent numerical fields, or linguistic writing.
They specify assumptions a proposed key must defend rather than establishing one.

The [hind-leg audit](HIND_LEG_HYPOTHESIS.md) tests the empirical coverage of an actual
published `taaL` proposal. It preserves the author's limited scope: no claim that
every occurrence must precede fish is imposed. Eight candidate occurrences supply
no independent semantic confirmation, and their direct P/M mapping is absent.
This is an unresolved reading hypothesis with concrete follow-up targets, not an
accepted decipherment or a falsified universal-adjacency rule.

The [quantity-anchor audit](QUANTITY_ANCHORS.md) supplies explicit countermodels
to unique numerical readings from a fitted capacity scale or a broad contents
interval. Preserve the physical measurement, sign reconstruction and proposed
numeral assignment as separate evidence layers when evaluating future examples.
The [vessel-capacity fit](CAPACITY_MODELS.md) adds provisional positive evidence
for proportional amounts in one selected three-pot group, with explicit failures
of transferring the same unit to other reported objects. Multiple-unit explanations
need independently defined classes before they can predict a new vessel's amount.

The [sign-family audit](SIGN_FAMILIES.md) tests all 76 multi-W source groupings and
retains a coverage row for every M sign. Twenty families meet its support gate;
context gains are small in aggregate and not uniformly robust to weighting and
smoothing. P004, P230 and P364 are exploratory image-review priorities, not accepted
allograph sets or established semantic distinctions. Any proposed inventory reduction
must preserve the full substitution witnesses and counterevidence.
The [all-code substitution network](SUBSTITUTION_NETWORK.md) now enumerates those
witnesses independently of graphic-family selection. Its short-context dominance,
cooccurrence and missing transitive edges prevent treating network components as
established classes. The complete graph and isolated-sign rows remain available
for testing any future proposed key.

## Candidate reading keys and catalogue identity

The [2026 candidate key audit](GLOSSA_KEY_AUDIT.md) adds a concrete prerequisite
for future phonetic tests: authenticate the sign namespace before interpreting a
fit score. The inspected M012/M047/M099/M328 pictorial premises contradict primary
forms. M059's fish-like form supplies no tally-count basis for its proposed seven,
while leaving an arbitrary numeral convention logically possible. No language
hypothesis is ruled out by these particular catalogue errors. Proposed values for
all supplied signs remain separately indexed as unvalidated external claims.

The [M77 recovery audit](M77_CANDIDATE_CORPUS.md) supplies direct M-number
sequences for further tests, with preserved uncertainty and explicit omissions.
Its primary2405 witness shows seven displayed entries collapsed to one sequence.
Correct codebook decoding and complete side/line recovery are prerequisites to
claiming that a rule fits every occurrence. M328's selected profile reverses the
dominant full-catalogue position, so roles learned from this subset must be tested
against the retained primary marginals rather than generalized automatically.

## Copper-tablet external associations

The [copper bridge audit](COPPER_BRIDGES.md) identifies concrete donor and motif
objects for a proposed text–image equivalence. W777 has multiple source motif
labels; its goat connection relies on Poor donors. A one-sign/one-animal-name rule
must account for these paths without silently merging or reclassifying inconvenient
objects. Image confirmation and new context are required before treating this as
semantic equivalence. No phonetic value is licensed by a shared-text bridge.


## Repetition and numerical attributes

The [all-sign repetition screen](REPETITION_EXPANSION.md) tests whether a maximal
run of two to four identical signs has an exact counterpart containing the
corresponding graphic stroke count followed by that sign. The primary fish-shaped
pair fits, but the same surrounding string permits two other replacements.
Shared placement therefore supplies no equality constraint on meanings or values.
Empty-frame matches are retained with their weaker specificity and object-type
confounds. The all417 coverage table leaves meanings unresolved.


## Bahrain name-list hypothesis: current boundary

The [Bahrain audit](BAHRAIN_SEAL_CROSSWALK.md) recovers Gelb's published reading
of three people, with the third identified as son of the first, from a rough
copy. It does not join any name to the Indus seal. No sound substitution test
is justified yet: selecting a name, segmenting the seal, choosing phonetic
values and then fitting the same name would be circular.

The discriminating next evidence is Brunswig–Parpola–Potts1983 BBVO2 p107,
plate3 fig12, plus Qala'at al-Bahrain1(1994) fig1715 and the find register.
These could clarify tablet identity and context; they would not automatically
establish bilingual equivalence. Catalogue figures1725/1726 identify the two
Qala'at seals in Laursen2010, but the closest seal has not been authenticated.
For the distinct 2026 tokens, full catalogue entries and plates are needed;
public first-page/abstract evidence contains no verified Indus correspondence.


## Require a consistent middle split before proposing a longer name chain

The [new exhaustive extension](CONSISTENT_CHAIN_RESULTS.md) distinguishes local
pair overlaps from a coherent three-inscription split. It rejects1,152 of1,483
apparent paths in the unchanged domestic sample. The remaining331 are not name
identifications: a non-kinship category model has consistent paths too. Only one
surviving path matches multi-sign segments on both sides of its middle string;
its catalogue rows2463,2302,4084 are now visually checked. It is a candidate for
future independent context/grammar testing, not a basis for assigning sounds.
Any larger network must additionally use compatible boundaries across different
triples; the present test does not establish that global condition.

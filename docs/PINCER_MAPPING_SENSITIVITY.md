# The M-897 exception and preservation sensitivity

The local correspondence W550→M216, W555→M222 remains the best-supported
working conversion among the four tested assignments. It is **not established
globally**. Its only incompatible row among the context-matched candidates is
M-897, a fragment whose original photograph cannot confidently resolve the
diagnostic strokes. Preserve that unresolved discrepancy; do not silently recode
the row or call it a demonstrated error.

## Authenticate the exception before interpreting it

The [primary review](M897_PRIMARY_REVIEW.md) verifies **FEM545=DK9111**
in Mackay's table printed386/Archive leaf408: steatite, typeB,
0.95×0.82×0.29in, block8, houseII, room18, level−16.2ft. The corresponding
impression is numbered545 on the retained plateXCVII/leaf207. This supplies
an independent original-report identity rather than guessing a split of the
secondary field `DK9111545`. The secondary label M-897 remains a qualified
alias because CISI2's own metadata was not checked.

The photo supports the broad comb–crossed-form–capped-fish–strokes–enclosure
sequence. Its small blurred reproduction and source fragment flag do not establish
whether the crossed form's diagnostic ends belong to W550, W555 or a damaged
version. No evidence justifies drawing in lost strokes or deleting surviving ones.

The parent also inspected Mahadevan's normalized line2545, printed75/PDF86.
It depicts the five forms encoded M267 M099 M065 M222 M254. The appendix
printed818/PDF827 explicitly maps FEM2–704 to references2002–2704 respectively,
so FEM545→2545 follows an original catalogue rule. This confirms that the
cross-catalogue discrepancy concerns the same FEM object, not just a similar
text found by a computer. Mahadevan's normalized drawing supports M222, while
the photo's fine geometry remains unresolved. A normalized catalogue decision
is evidence to retain, not a substitute for clear physical strokes.

## Four fixed conversion hypotheses

The protocol was saved after observing M-897's fragment flag but before the
new partition counts. It is a source-exposed sensitivity exercise. For each
Wells row, a model fits only when one **whole** candidate M77 sequence satisfies
all target occurrences together. Positions cannot be cherry-picked from
different candidate texts. A row with no candidate is uninformative, not a
counterexample to every model.

M-897 is the one incompatible row for the first assignment among the36 rows
that have candidates. It drops out when applying the literal preservation label
`complete`. This is a filtering sensitivity, not a resolution of its reading.
The assignments share the same restricted target alphabet and incomplete
background crosswalk. These are compatibility counts, not accuracy, statistical
significance or independently identified artifact counts.

The source-complete subset is not a certified intact sample: **it still includes
L-9**, whose prior primary review showed a broken comb corner. That observation
is attached to its row without retroactively changing the specified selection.
Earlier M-63 provides strong primary comparison despite being labelled slightly
chipped and excluded by this particular source flag. Filtering cannot replace
artifact review. The fragment exception also cannot simply be dismissed because
removing it makes a favored rule fit.

## What may reasonably be concluded

Use the local split as a clearly labelled candidate conversion for further
discovery. Keep W550 and W555 separate and preserve each original inscription's
uncertainty. Do not overwrite P230, merge M216/M222, or assign phonetic/semantic
values. The within-object M-63 evidence is stronger than compatible contexts;
M-897 needs a sharper authenticated image or direct object inspection before
its discrepancy can be resolved. Many unmatched rows may instead expose missing
crosswalk entries or different token boundaries.

The [audit script](../scripts/audit_pincer_preservation.py),
[protocol](../data/processed/pincer_preservation_protocol.json) and
[full row results](../results/pincer_preservation_audit.json) retain every
compatible, incompatible and uninformative row. A test guards against assembling
a fictitious compatible text from positions belonging to different candidates.
The independent verifier reconstructs whole-sequence model assignments and
metadata partitions without importing the production helper functions.
No new established meaning or sound is assigned to any of the417 signs.

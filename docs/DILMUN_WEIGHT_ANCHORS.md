# Dilmun and imported-weight anchor check

**No direct Indus-sign/value anchor was established among three reviewed weights.** The test requires the same identified object to carry demonstrable Indus signs and an independently established value. A measured mass, foreign shape or separate cuneiform conversion account does not meet that condition.

| Object | Reported mass | Evidence and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ur lot **2111** | **27.4 g** | Chert cube, 25 mm. The published face has no readable inscription. Its assignment to two 13.7 g units follows shape and mass. The disturbed findspot prevents secure attribution to Abisum's household. |
| Ur **U.18699E** | **13.51 g**, rounded to 13.5 g elsewhere | Duck weight reportedly marked **1½ units** in Mesopotamian fashion. A possible connection to the Indus standard is proposed, but no Indus signs are reported. The original marked surface was not independently inspected. |
| Qala'at al-Bahrain **520.TH**, object III | **27 g** | Polished chert cube; found about 20 m from the main gate-building group. No inscription is reported or readable in the small group photograph. Its Harappan comparison is metrological. |

The Ur evidence comes from the excavators' [2023 study](https://www.vorderas-archaeologie.uni-muenchen.de/personen/professoren/otto/publikationen/hafford-einwag-otto_ur_weights.pdf), printed180,192–196,198 (Fig9, footnote29, Table1). The duck's numerical marking is an author-reported observation, not a newly verified transcription; the earlier Hafford2012 article could not be accessed directly.

[Bibby's report](https://tidsskrift.dk/kuml/article/view/105436), printed345–347, supplies the Bahrain photograph, identifier and mass. Its **UET V796** comparison concerns a separate cuneiform tablet recording Ur/Dilmun weight conversions. That tablet is not an inscription on 520.TH and is not an Indus bilingual. It cannot fix the value of M328 or another Indus sign.

The structured review is `data/processed/dilmun_weight_anchors.json`. Three public source downloads, their URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes are in `data/processed/dilmun_weight_downloads.json`; the central registry was not edited. Locally rendered pages retain visual evidence. A single shown face cannot establish that every surface is uninscribed. This negative result applies to the three reviewed cases only.

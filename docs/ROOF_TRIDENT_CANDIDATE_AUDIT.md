# W391 source candidates versus native M163 records

The retained Lipi CSV contains **ten W391 rows**. Four have documentary chains to already known native-M77 M163 objects; a fifth has a qualified candidate chain. Five further W-coded rows are additional candidates relative to that five-reference target set, but one is already the native2923 tang with an unreadable initial span, two historical objects remain unjoined, and two external-source candidates await separate primary review. **No new native M163 attestation or new independent physical artifact is authenticated by this audit.**

P089 lists W391 with M163 as a candidate cross-catalogue association. It is not a global conversion rule. In particular, **W391 is not M391**: the identical number belongs to different sign schemes. The M391 codes elsewhere in the metal-tool lines must not be confused with the target M163.

## Native target coverage and what “new” means

Scanning the retained native candidate export for M163 returns exactly1051,1397,2119,2925 and8054. These are existing records, not new discoveries from W391. The documentary joins do not arise from matching sequences: they use field/registration identifiers and explicit old-publication rules. The2119 candidate remains weaker because its raw identifier split is not independently completed here.

“Additional candidate to this native-target selection” applies to the other five W rows; it does **not** mean five objects absent from M77. DK7856 already has reference2923. DK7854 and M1384 have unresolved native identities. DHR-24475 and Ns-53 are plausible additional source attestations requiring the separately assigned primary review, not newly verified M163 tokens. A global search proving their absence under every M77 alias was not performed. Their physical newness and independence therefore remain unestablished here.

The records are already present in the downloaded W dataset, so none is a newly downloaded raw record. A genuinely new usable observation for a future roof study would need an authenticated object/field, a source-level roof determination, eligible completeness and uncertainty, and independent object/die or formula grouping. Merely importing a W391 code cannot supply those conditions.

## Metal-tool disagreement must remain explicit

The existing [arch catalogue audit](ARCH_EXTENSION_CATALOGUE.md) authenticates2925 as CXXVI5 and its first side as:

```
M163 M252 M261 M134 *M098 M391 M391
```

The second side is separate and is not appended to this field. The W row for the axe has six tokens. Neither equal text length nor complete tokenwise equivalence is established across the catalogues, and the M098 doubt remains even though the target M163 is not itself starred.

For the [already identified tang2923](BRONZE2923_IDENTITY.md), primary printed85/PDF96 visibly begins with a hatched lost span on the first face. The export preserves:

```
LOSS M252 M261 M134 *M098 M391 *M391
```

Its other face has the independently audited stroke/arch sequence. The W row supplies a terminal source-order W391 in the similar six-token string, but that does not read the missing initial M77 group. It is a source-representation discrepancy, not evidence that M77’s loss should be repaired or that both tools have identical complete inscriptions. No conclusion about the physical roof on the tang follows without adequate primary evidence.

The unnumbered DK7854/CXXXI37 row is not rescued by the shared W sequence. The earlier [plate audit](METAL_PLATE_AUDIT.md) marked the photograph joins tentative, and its comparison of37 with the small axe did not authenticate a field-number chain. This audit leaves7854 literal and makes no replacement assignment. Likewise the original7535 versus later7835 discrepancy for the large axe and the blurred digit in the tang’s original field number remain documented, rather than being silently repaired.

## Source inspection and reproduction

Primary images inspected in this bounded pass were M77 Appendix printed818/PDF827 and827/PDF836; M77 Texts printed85/PDF96; the existing2925 crop; CISI printed374/PDF410; Marshall volumeII printed402/PDF53; and Mackay’s existing original table crop from printed489. A new local render of retained Marshall PDF55, printed404, authenticated row397=VS3227. It used the unique `roof-trident-marshall` prefix and downloaded nothing.

Existing original-tool, arch and identity reviews supply the qualified plate/item and uncertainty findings. A search of the retained Mackay OCR did not recover a reliable exact7854 or10551 bridge. OCR weakness is not absence evidence; no wider download or speculative identity repair was attempted. DHR/Nausharo photo assignment remains outside this subtask.

The reproducible selector is `scripts/audit_roof_trident_candidates.py`. Its manual identity dispositions are explicit, separate from mechanical extraction. It writes `data/processed/roof_trident_candidate_audit.json`, preserving all ten source rows, native candidate flags, exact source paths and SHA256/byte pins. It does not modify the W or M corpus, frozen roof tests, or previous source reviews. All semantic and phonetic values remain unassigned.

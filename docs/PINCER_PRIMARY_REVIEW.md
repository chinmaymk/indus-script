# Two crossed-sign forms on one seal: M-63

M-63 supplies a checked artifact containing both forms that the secondary export
calls W550 and W555. Its primary normalized transcription distinguishes M216
and M222. This strengthens the finding that the P230 family listing cannot be
used as a lossless conversion of both Wells codes to M216. It does not prove
different sounds or meanings for the forms.

## Identity established independently of text matching

The CISI India volume, printed366/PDF402, lists M-63, Finnish0223,
excavation **DK8333**, museum **ASI63.10.12**, original photo **HU98** and
impression reference **Si.21 p.1:215**. Its photographs are printed28/PDF64.
Mahadevan's appendix printed819/PDF828 independently lists **text3068,
DK8333, Si.21:215**. Thus the shared excavation and photograph references
establish the bridge. Finnish0223 is not mistaken for a Mahadevan text number.

Mahadevan text3068 is printed87/PDF98. Its normalized spatial left-to-right
order is comb, outward-hooked crossed form, stroke/curve form, inward-hooked
crossed form. The candidate export's reading-order sequence is
**M216 M123 M222 M254**. The Wells export for M-63, row2591.1, has opposite
file order: **W526 W555 W060 W550**. No numerical value is assigned to W060
or M123 in this review; their shape is only an intervening identifier.

## What the photograph supports

The paired original and impression show four groups above the animal. Both
crossed forms survive in the same inscription and are separated by the
stroke/curve form. The open arm ends of the crossed form nearest the comb
differ from the terminal crossed form at the other end. The distinction is
visible in the original and compatible with the normalized M222/M216 contrast
in the primary sign list (printed33–34/PDF44–45). Mirrored views are one
artifact, not two independent attestations.

The comb is also interesting: the original's comb is at spatial right; the
impression's is at spatial left. Its upper and lower extending stems survive.
There is surface wear and minor edge irregularity, but no conspicuous missing
comb corner of the kind seen on L-9. Its orientation differs from the L-89
comb in like-for-like impression view. This supports retaining the W526/W527
graphic-orientation distinction for study. It does not establish interchangeability,
since M-63 and L-89 have different surrounding texts. Mahadevan normalizes the
comb under M254; that choice is not proof of identical linguistic value.

The source marks M-63 slightly chipped and complete=Y. No claim of perfect
preservation follows. Broad form/order observations are more secure than a
claim to have independently certified every microscopic stroke. No original
object was physically examined.

## Consequences and limits

On this artifact, the direct correspondence supports W550↔M216 and
W555↔M222. L-89 separately supports the latter correspondence. A universal
conversion still needs more primary shared-object checks: one inventory may
split distinctions another groups, and tokenization can differ. Co-occurrence
on one seal rules out explaining the entire observed difference solely by
different objects or sites; positional allography, scribal variation and distinct
sign identity remain possible. No semantic or phonetic assignment follows.

The previously verified primary Table I (printed720/PDF731) also preserves
different position profiles; these are historical catalogue counts, not newly
discovered texts:

| Sign | Alone | Initial | Medial | Final | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| M216 | 2 | 36 | 49 | 3 | 90 |
| M222 | 0 | 2 | 20 | 0 | 22 |

M222 is predominantly medial in these counts. That is compatible with a
position-dependent variant as well as a separate sign with restricted use.
The distribution alone does not choose between those explanations.

The [context screen](PINCER_CONTEXT_SCREEN.md) searches for candidate shared
contexts using the remaining source-family memberships. It is a discovery aid,
not a new corpus of authenticated shared artifacts. It must not be used to
turn this selected primary example into a claimed global success rate.

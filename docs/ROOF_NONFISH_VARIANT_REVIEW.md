# Non-fish roof-pair variants

Mahadevan’s documented variants retain **roof absence for M137 and M162 and roof presence for M334**. They do not preserve an identical underlying drawing. M162 varies particularly strongly in arm geometry and upper prong count. The representative pairs therefore remain qualified visual comparisons; the Appendix does not establish a universal exact “add a roof” operation.

This is a bounded manual inspection of three assigned code rows, conducted with the representative hypotheses already known. No sequence distributions, context counts, translations or new meanings were examined. Mahadevan’s grouping is editorial evidence, not proof that the ancient forms had the same meaning.

The reference labels identify the examples printed under the variant drawings. They are not independently authenticated physical-object joins in this review. The counts above count Appendix drawings, not corpus occurrences.

The six existing representative crops M137/M138, M162/M163 and M333/M334 were also inspected. They agree with the earlier representative-level comparisons. The Appendix adds an essential limit: one cannot apply the representative deletion or addition to every code occurrence and assume the same exact base. For M334, the broad roof remains recognizable despite the change below it. For M137, absence of roof is retained despite extra arm marks. For M162, the broad branching/stem family is much less specific than the representative U-trident.

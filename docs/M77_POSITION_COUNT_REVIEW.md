# Mahadevan positional counts and omitted U-final lines

**Table I counts positions within individual lines. Starred doubtful readings belong to the catalogue's legible-sign inventory. The inspected definitions do not supply an explicit damaged-edge allocation algorithm.** Removing loss markers and treating the surviving tokens as complete lines would therefore add an unverified rule.

This review used the existing Mahadevan 1977 PDF and local canonical export. [The ledger](../data/processed/m77_position_count_review.json) preserves hashes, inspected images, definitions and complete digital witness records. No downloads or corpus-wide residual calculations occurred.

## Primary definitions verified visually

| Locator | Verified definition |
|---|---|
| Printed 10/PDF 21, §9.3 | Line is the statistical unit because continuity and true order among lines on the same object or side cannot be assumed. |
| Printed 18/PDF 29, §11.11 | Solus means one sign constituting the whole line; the other categories are initial, medial and final. |
| Printed 19/PDF 30, §12.1 | Asterisk marks doubtful reading. A loss block can represent one or more signs of uncountable extent. |
| Printed 21/PDF 32, §14.3(ii) | The concordance lists every legible occurrence, expressly including starred doubtful signs. |
| Printed 21/PDF 32, §14.3(iv) | Concordance sorting uses positions within the whole text; this differs from Table I's unit. |
| Printed 23/PDF 34, §15.2 | Table I uses a line; whole-text statistics can instead be gathered from the concordance. |
| Printed 723/PDF 734, Table I notes | Defines the column abbreviations and reiterates the line unit. No damaged-edge algorithm is given. |

The totals appear both in §11.11 and Table I: solus 190, initial 3,010, medial 7,196, final 2,976; total 13,372. The total matches the stated inventory of legible occurrences. Including doubtful readings in that inventory is explicit in the concordance definition; exact table reconstruction should retain them or label their exclusion as a subset analysis.

These inspected passages do **not** explicitly say whether a known sign adjacent to a lost edge is classified as initial/final or medial, nor whether one surviving sign plus damage can count as solus. Do not infer those rules from `000` serialization. Primary totals may be subtracted from validated retained counts only under aligned sign identity, multiplicity, line unit and positional conventions. Complete, independently delimited lines avoid the unresolved damaged-edge convention. A numerical residual is conditional on those checks.

None of these five omitted lines has a visible asterisk or loss block. Their inclusion as separate U-final lines needs no reconstruction of missing signs or concatenation to another side. The five witnesses demonstrate a concrete mechanism for losing U-final occurrences from a one-row-per-object export; they are not a random sample or an estimate of the total loss.

Printed 615/PDF 626 actually indexes **M342**, retaining these U-final lines as context. Printed 581/PDF 592 indexes **M328** itself. This distinction is recorded to prevent an OCR-derived mistaken concordance range.

Damaged witnesses remain separate: text 4639 side 2 and text 5456 side 2, printed 564/PDF 575, contain plain U beside a loss block. This review does not turn them into complete one-sign lines or restore any supposed preceding strokes.

# Primary glyph checks: M77 9801 and 9822

Both candidate arrays agree with a bounded visual reading of Mahadevan's Texts, printed page 160 / PDF page 171, checked against the labelled sign list (printed pages 32–35 / PDF pages 43–46).

| Reference | Normalized reading order | Primary direction code | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9801 | M173 M010 M097 M121 M185 M097 M297 M057 | 2, left to right | Eight proposed IDs supported |
| 9822 | M173 M403 M123 M102 M059 M249 M162 M342 | 1, right to left | Eight proposed IDs supported |

Both are seals with only side 0 and only line 0. Neither printed row marks a doubtful sign or a lost passage. The printed Texts layout is normalized: reading its glyphs right to left reproduces the arrays above. The direction code 2 on 9801 does not require reversing this normalized sequence.

For 9801, the two M097 short strokes are separately positioned above the baseline, between M010/M121 and M185/M297. M121 has twelve short strokes in three rows of four. M185 has the grid form matching the labelled sign, rather than the adjacent ladder-like variant. The leftmost complex fish form supports M057.

For 9822, the principal ambiguity considered was whether its three parallel strokes should be M102 (short) or M089 (long). The displayed form matches M102 in the labelled list. The concordance at printed page 346 / PDF page 357 also places 9822 as the final indexed row under the three-short-stroke form; PDF page 358 continues that index and supplies clear visual contrasts between short and long groups. These are internal catalogue checks, not independent measurements of the artifact.

This review authenticates two catalogue transcriptions only. It does not validate the fidelity of the original drawings, all other exported lines, language, quantity readings, or manufacture abroad.

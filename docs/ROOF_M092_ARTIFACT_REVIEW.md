# M092 on Kalibangan K-29: artifact and identity review

27 September 2026. **M77 text 8018 is securely joined to CISI K-29 through excavation number 8219 and museum registration ASI 68.1.48.** Published photographs show an angular roof above three long parallel strokes on the surviving seal fragment. The inscription field is substantially broken; this recovery does not make it eligible for the complete-field modifier test.

## What the photographs establish

In CISI K-29 A and its impression a, an apex-up angular mark is visible above three long parallel incisions. Two shorter adjacent strokes survive toward the broken side; they appear to the left of the roofed group in the impression. The roof is visible as a separate upper graphic element rather than being supplied solely by a modern normalized drawing. The excavation report's color obverse/impression corroborates that observation. These are different views/publications of **one object**, not independent attestations.

A large irregular break removes the rest of the field on the left in impression orientation and cuts into the region around the adjacent strokes and animal. The photographs cannot supply the number or identity of missing signs, nor guarantee the original lengths of strokes meeting broken margins. Nothing here converts visible stroke count into an ancient quantity.

The excavation description states that only one edge remains intact (27 mm), thickness is 7.5 mm, the white steatite seal has a white coating, and the reverse is too damaged for its boss to be made out. It records KLB-2, the baulk between Squares XA6 and XB6, Layer 1, a late level of Period II. This is published excavation provenance for this object; no site label in the general secondary corpus was silently changed. The author says the two short strokes may have accompanied the roofed group; that is an interpretation, not an established unit or meaning.

The [earlier normalized-field review](ROOF_M092_COVERAGE_REVIEW.md) checked `M092 M099 LOSS` in M77. The physical break now supports keeping the entire field excluded. The unroofed M089 comparison is not recovered on this object, and this single roofed attestation supplies no complete cross-root analogy rectangle.

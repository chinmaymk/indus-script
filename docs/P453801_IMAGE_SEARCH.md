# P453801 / Nisaba 15/2 no. 371: primary image-source search

**An authenticated primary handcopy was found, but it does not depict the seal.** Its explicit caption is “Seal illegible.” No photograph of this exact artifact or readable seal drawing was located in this bounded search. The seal's script and any connection to an Indus sequence remain unidentified.

The saved [handcopy](../data/raw/bdtns_192941_owen_handcopy.jpg) shows two cuneiform tablet-face drawings, followed by that caption. There is no separate seal panel, motif or sequence to transcribe. This is a drawing, not an artifact photograph. The caption records an editorial judgement; it does not prove that better photography could never recover seal details.

## Exact source chain

The existing official CDLI page for **P453801** links **BDTNS 192941** and identifies the primary publication as **Owen 2013, Nisaba 15, no. 371**. The [BDTNS catalogue](https://bdtns.cesga.es/192941) links back to that CDLI identifier and explicitly lists **Nisaba 15/2 0371** as an edition containing a handcopy and transliteration. Its image link points to `U192941_1.jpg`, with an attribution to a handcopy from that exact catalogue entry, drawn by David I. Owen and copyrighted by CDL Press. The match is established by those explicit cross-references, rather than guessed from similar names or cuneiform shapes.

Downloaded evidence is preserved in [the catalogue HTML](../data/raw/bdtns_192941_catalogue.html) and [the linked handcopy](../data/raw/bdtns_192941_owen_handcopy.jpg). The publication is *Cuneiform Texts Primarily from Iri-Saĝrig / Āl-Šarrākī and the History of the Ur III Period*, volume II, *Catalogue and Texts*, Nisaba 15, CDL Press, 2013. [Official CDLI publication record](https://cdli.earth/publications/110334). ## What remains unresolved

- `seal 1` is a local section label. The generic CDLI dummy record P533185/S999999 does not identify a real common seal.
- `Anonymous 453801` is a catalogue label, not a verified institutional accession or seal owner's name.
- Names of recipients in the tablet body do not identify its sealer. No bilingual or name-to-Indus-sign alignment is supplied by the drawing.
- BDTNS notes possible textual identity with CUSAS 40/2 no. 1582, while warning that the year-names differ. This image search does not establish either physical identity or physical distinctness of those records.
- The actual CDLI image area in the saved HTML has a no-image placeholder. Its generic social-image metadata is not proof that a usable photograph exists.

The next decisive evidence would be a photograph or seal-impression drawing explicitly tied to this artifact. The present primary drawing corroborates the unresolved seal reading rather than resolving it. No phonetic value, translation, museum identity or Indus classification has been inferred.

No large publication scan or duplicate CDLI page was downloaded. Two new raw files were saved: the BDTNS catalogue and its explicitly linked handcopy. No canonical corpus or shared source registry was edited.

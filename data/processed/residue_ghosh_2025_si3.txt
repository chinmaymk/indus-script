Methodology
The open porosity derived from the 
Karanpura
 ceramics was grounded on Archimedes’ principle of liquid immersion. The technique used for measuring open porosity in ceramics involved the use
of a density kit, and the final calculation was executed using the formula mentioned below:
Open Porosity (
%)=
( ww-
dw
)/ww-
iw
)×100 
The initials mentioned in the above formula stands for 
ww: 
wet weight
 
dw
: 
dry weight 
 
iw
: 
immersed weight
Initially, the ceramic sample was cut into smaller pieces using a specialized ceramic cutting machine, followed by the recording of the mass of the dry sample. Next, the sample was soaked in distilled water for an hour until they reached their full saturation level. Following this, the mass of the wet sample was evaluated, and finally, the process was wrapped up with the recording of the mass of the immersed samples from 
Karanpura
.
